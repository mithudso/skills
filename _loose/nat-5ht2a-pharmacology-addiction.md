# 5-HT2A Receptor Pharmacology in Addiction

## Core Concepts
- **5-HT2A**: Gq-coupled GPCR densest in cortical layer V pyramidal neurons; also high in striatum, amygdala, and default mode network (DMN) nodes — all addiction-relevant circuits
- **Full agonists** (LSD, DMT, psilocin): maximal Gq + β-arrestin activation → hallucinogenic + potential therapeutic; **partial agonists** (psilocybin prodrug → psilocin): similar profile at lower intrinsic activity
- **Antagonists** (ketanserin, volinanserin/M100907): block both hallucinogenic and most reward-potentiating effects of stimulants/opioids
- **Biased agonism**: compounds that preferentially activate Gq over β-arrestin (or vice versa) may dissociate psychedelic from therapeutic effects — actively contested
- **TrkB co-engagement**: psychedelics directly bind BDNF receptor TrkB at a transmembrane allosteric site independent of 5-HT2A, contributing to neuroplasticity outcomes

## Mechanisms / Biology
- **Gq → PLC cascade**: IP3 + DAG → Ca²⁺ release + PKC activation → immediate excitatory cortical effects, head-twitch response (rodent psychedelic proxy), and altered salience coding
- **β-arrestin pathway**: receptor internalization/desensitization; proposed to mediate some antidepressant-like effects but challenged by 2025 evidence that overall signaling *efficacy*, not bias ratio, predicts psychedelic vs. non-psychedelic outcomes
- **Habit-reversal**: 5-HT2A agonism in prefrontal cortex and striatum disrupts overlearned drug-cue associations; a single psilocybin dose reduces conditioned oxycodone behavior in male mice via 5-HT2A (Nature Communications, 2025) — sex-specific (females unaffected)
- **Craving modulation**: antagonism of 5-HT2A attenuates methamphetamine-induced reward and anxiety-like behavior in rats; serotonergic tone modulates cue-induced craving via orbitofrontal-amygdala circuitry
- **Neuroplasticity**: TrkB activation by psychedelics promotes dendritic spine growth and BDNF signaling — proposed mechanism for durable (weeks–months) behavioral change after single-session dosing
- **DMN disruption**: PET studies show psilocybin 10 mg/70 kg achieves ~39.5% mean cortical 5-HT2A occupancy; DMN nodes (subgenual ACC, angular gyri) reach 63–75% occupancy, correlating with ego-dissolution and therapeutic response

## Clinical Picture
- **SUD applications**: alcohol use disorder (AUD), opioid use disorder (OUD), tobacco dependence — all have published pilot RCT or Phase II data with psilocybin-assisted therapy
- **Alcohol**: meta-analysis of LSD RCTs shows consistent medium-sized effect on abstinence; psilocybin Phase II (NYU/Johns Hopkins) reduced heavy drinking days significantly at 8-month follow-up
- **Opioid**: psilocybin 25 mg reduced opioid craving scores and anxiety in Phase I/II; preclinical 5-HT2A-dependence confirmed; clinical sex-difference data awaited
- **Tobacco**: 67–80% abstinence at 6-month follow-up in open-label psilocybin studies (Hopkins); far above standard pharmacotherapy benchmarks (~35%)
- **Prevalence context**: ~48 million Americans meet SUD criteria annually; existing pharmacotherapy leaves >70% without adequate long-term remission

## Evidence & Treatment
- **Psilocybin occupancy threshold**: ≥60% 5-HT2A occupancy in DMN nodes required for full psychedelic (and likely therapeutic) response; low-dose "sub-psychedelic" regimens may be insufficient for habit-reversal
- **Antagonist strategy**: 5-HT2A antagonists (ketanserin, quetiapine) reduce stimulant reward sensitization and may blunt relapse-precipitating cue reactivity — tested adjunctively in stimulant SUD
- **Functional selectivity challenge**: efforts to develop non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A agonists (e.g., tabernanthalog, AAZ-A-154) have mixed preclinical results; 2025 data suggest psychedelic effect may be inseparable from therapeutic Gq efficacy
- **Set-and-setting**: clinical benefit in psychedelic-assisted therapy is not pharmacology alone; 5-HT2A-mediated neuroplasticity window (~4–6 hours) must be paired with structured psychotherapy
- **Regulatory status**: psilocybin is Schedule I (US); FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation for MDD and AUD trials active; Oregon and Colorado state-level regulated access ongoing as of 2026

## Key Facts
- Mean cortical 5-HT2A occupancy at psilocybin 10 mg/70 kg: **~39.5%**; DMN peak nodes: **63–75%**
- Psilocybin tobacco cessation 6-month abstinence rate: **~67–80%** vs. ~35% for varenicline
- LSD alcohol RCT meta-analysis effect size: **medium** (Cohen's d ~0.6) on abstinence outcomes
- 5-HT2A receptor densest in **cortical layer V** (pyramidal cells); second highest in striatum
- Sex difference: psilocybin 5-HT2A-mediated opioid extinction demonstrated in **male** but **not female** mice (2025); clinical implications under investigation

# Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy Protocol Design

## Core Concepts
- **Three-phase structure**: All major trials (MAPS, NYU, Hopkins) share prep sessions → drug session(s) → integration sessions; the non-drug phases are considered active treatment, not mere bookends
- **Set and setting**: "Set" = participant mindset/intentions; "setting" = physical environment (comfortable room, eye shades, curated music, therapist presence); both are manualized in modern trials to standardize non-pharmacological variables
- **Therapist dyad model**: Two therapists (one male/one female preferred in MAPS protocol) remain continuously present during the dosing session; roles are non-directive — support over interpretation, "inner healer" model
- **Drug-session duration**: MDMA session ~6–8 hours; psilocybin session ~6–8 hours; both require on-site observation throughout due to altered-state duration
- **Integration**: Post-session psychotherapy helps participants process experiences and translate insights into behavior change; typically 2–4 weekly sessions per drug session

## Mechanisms / Biology
- Psilocybin's active metabolite psilocin binds 5-HT2A serotonin receptors with high affinity, producing default-mode-network (DMN) disruption and increased neuroplasticity
- MDMA acts as monoamine releaser/reuptake inhibitor (serotonin > dopamine > norepinephrine), producing anxiolysis and increased prosocial openness that facilitates trauma processing
- FDA granted Breakthrough Therapy designation to psilocybin (Compass Pathways, Usona) and MDMA (Lykos/MAPS) based on phase 2 data; FDA declined to approve MDMA-AT in August 2024 and requested a new phase 3 trial
- Neuroplasticity window: psychedelics upregulate BDNF and promote dendritic spine growth in PFC, with the integration period theorized to be a critical window for new learning
- Mystical-experience intensity (measured by MEQ-30) correlates with therapeutic outcomes across psilocybin depression and addiction trials

## Clinical Picture
- **PTSD**: MAPS phase 3 MAPP1/MAPP2 trials — 67% of MDMA-AT participants no longer met PTSD diagnostic criteria vs. 32% placebo (Mithoefer et al.); effect size Cohen's d ≈ 0.91
- **Treatment-resistant depression**: Compass COMP360 phase 2b — 25 mg psilocybin showed significant reduction in MADRS at 3 weeks vs. 1 mg control; NYU and Hopkins trials showed rapid antidepressant effects
- **Alcohol use disorder**: Hopkins trial (Barrett et al.) — 2 psilocybin sessions + 12 sessions psychotherapy: 83% reduction in heavy drinking days vs. 51% diphenhydramine control
- Indications explored: MDD, PTSD, AUD, OUD, tobacco cessation (~80% abstinence at 6 months, Hopkins n=15), cancer-related existential distress
- Switzerland program: ~100 authorized physicians treated 723 patients with MDMA/LSD/psilocybin; ~1,660 treatments conducted in 2024 under legal limited-use authorization

## Evidence & Treatment
- **MAPS MDMA-AT protocol**: 2 preparatory sessions (~90 min each) + 2–3 MDMA sessions (80–120 mg + optional 40–60 mg supplemental dose at ~1.5 hrs) + 3 integration sessions per drug session; total ~42 contact hours
- **Hopkins/NYU psilocybin**: Single or two high-dose sessions (25–30 mg/70 kg), preceded by 3–5 prep sessions (~8 hrs total) and 2–3 integration sessions; room design includes couch, music playlist, and art
- **Fidelity scales**: MAPS uses the COMPASS fidelity rating system; raters code therapist adherence to non-directiveness, support quality, and protocol deviations; systematic reviews note poor fidelity reporting across most published trials
- **Functional unblinding**: In MAPS phase 3, 94% of MDMA recipients correctly identified assignment; 75% of placebo recipients also correctly guessed; this violates standard double-blind assumptions and is the primary methodological critique of the field
- **Control-arm challenge**: Active placebos (niacin, low-dose drug) partially mitigate but don't eliminate unblinding; expectancy inflation in open-label arms remains unquantified; some analysts argue reported effect sizes are upper-bound estimates

## Key Facts
- MDMA phase 3 MAPP2: 71.2% response rate (≥30% CAPS-5 reduction) for MDMA-AT vs. 47.6% placebo
- Psilocybin dose range in trials: 10–30 mg fixed or 0.1–0.4 mg/kg weight-adjusted; 25 mg is the emerging "standard" high dose
- Therapist training: MAPS requires ~100-hour training program including personal MDMA experience; Hopkins requires therapists to have personal psychedelic experience
- Integration session timing: most critical in first 72 hours post-session when neuroplasticity window is theorized to be open
- Adverse events: cardiovascular (MDMA hypertension), anxiety/overwhelming experience ("difficult trip"), and rare cases of therapist boundary violations noted in MAPS program audit (2023)

# Modafinil and Armodafinil: SUD-Context Safety

## Mechanism (SUD-relevant summary)
- Atypical DAT inhibitor: occupies DAT at ~30–35% at therapeutic doses (PET-confirmed in cocaine abusers); induces outward-facing DAT conformation — slower kinetics than amphetamine/cocaine, attenuated reinforcement signal
- Does NOT cause rapid striatal dopamine surge (no transporter substrate activity); this is the mechanistic basis for lower abuse liability
- Full pharmacology → narc-modafinil-armodafinil-pharmacology

## DEA Schedule IV
- Moderate abuse potential; requires prescription, no refills without new Rx in most states; not Schedule II (no controlled-substance triplicate in most states)
- Appropriate caution warranted — not high-risk like amphetamines — but real schedule, real potential

## Drug-Liking Data
- Controlled lab studies: modafinil at therapeutic doses produces measurable but modest drug-liking — significantly above placebo, well below amphetamine or cocaine
- Dose-dependent: 400 mg produces more euphoria than 200 mg; 200 mg is the usual clinical ceiling
- Cocaine and meth abusers in SUD studies: modafinil did not serve as a positive reinforcer (not self-administered above placebo rates) — key finding for SUD prescribing
- Modafinil blunts cocaine-induced euphoria in three independent controlled human lab studies (possible benefit in cocaine SUD; results not replicated to clinical significance)
- Contrast: pitolisant shows drug-liking indistinguishable from placebo in abuse-potential studies → preferred in active SUD

## SUD-Specific Misuse Patterns
- Oral dose escalation (taking 2–3× prescribed dose for alertness or euphoria enhancement)
- Combining with stimulants (cocaine, meth, MDMA) to potentiate euphoria — primary risk in active SUD
- Snorting: attempted but poor bioavailability sublingually/intranasally; modafinil is not water-soluble — atypical misuse route
- Dual identity problem: modafinil was extensively trialed as a meth/cocaine SUD treatment (see nat-modafinil-agonist-replacement-strategy); meth patients may have prior non-prescribed exposure or awareness of its effects

## Prescribing in Meth/Stimulant Recovery
**General principle:** Untreated narcolepsy-level EDS substantially increases relapse risk (fatigue, cognitive impairment, impulsive decisions); treatment benefit can outweigh modest misuse risk in stable recovery.

**When acceptable:**
- Stable recovery ≥6 months (no active stimulant use)
- Clear narcolepsy/secondary narcolepsy diagnosis (not just fatigue)
- Shared decision-making: patient acknowledges schedule and misuse risks
- Willing to accept prescription monitoring program (PMP) checks and pill counts

**Prescription safeguards:**
- Start at 100 mg (morning); titrate to 200 mg only if needed; 400 mg rarely justified
- Morning-only dosing; avoid afternoon doses (reduces dose-seeking, insomnia)
- 30-day supply maximum; no early refills
- PMP check at each prescription; urine drug screen at baseline and periodic
- Document EDS severity (ESS score) at baseline; re-assess — if no EDS improvement, stop

**Red flags for discontinuation:**
- Requests for dose increases without documented EDS worsening
- Lost/stolen prescriptions
- UDS positive for non-prescribed stimulants
- Early refill requests

## Armodafinil vs. Modafinil in SUD Context
- R-enantiomer; longer half-life allows **single morning dose** — reduces number of daily dosing decision points (less dose-seeking opportunity) vs. modafinil's twice-daily option
- Abuse potential similar; no controlled data showing armodafinil safer in SUD specifically
- Some prescribers prefer armodafinil in SUD for the single-dose convenience and adherence simplicity
- 150 mg armodafinil ≈ 200 mg modafinil for wakefulness; 250 mg rarely justified

## Comparative Agent Summary for Narcolepsy in SUD/Recovery

| Agent | SUD Context | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pitolisant | Preferred in active SUD / MAT | No schedule; drug-liking = placebo |
| Modafinil / Armodafinil | Acceptable in stable recovery (≥6 mo) | Schedule IV; modest misuse potential; safeguards required |
| Solriamfetol | Use with caution; more than modafinil | Schedule IV; NET/DAT; higher drug-liking signal than modafinil |
| Amphetamines / methylphenidate | Avoid in stimulant SUD | Schedule II; high relapse risk |
| Sodium oxybate | With REMS + active monitoring only | GHB precursor; blackout/diversion risk; specialized programs |

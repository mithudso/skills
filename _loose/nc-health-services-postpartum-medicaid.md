# NC Medicaid 12-Month Postpartum Extension

## Overview

North Carolina extended Medicaid postpartum health care coverage from 60 days to **12 months** for eligible beneficiaries beginning **April 1, 2022**. This was among the first cohort of states to implement the American Rescue Plan (ARP) Act option, which created a 5-year state plan amendment pathway for extended postpartum coverage.

**Legal authority:**
- Federal: American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (ARP) Section 9812 — creates the option for states to extend postpartum coverage to 12 months via SPA
- State: NC Senate Bill 105 / Session Law 2021-180 Section 9D.13 — NC General Assembly authorization
- CMS-approved NC State Plan Amendment; authority in effect through **March 31, 2027**
- Consolidated Appropriations Act 2023 (CAA 2023) made 12-month postpartum coverage a **permanent Medicaid option** (no longer sunset at 2027 for ongoing state plan authority)

## Who Is Eligible

The extension applies to **all categories of beneficiaries** in all NC Medicaid programs, including:

- Medicaid for Pregnant Women (MPW) enrollees
- Expansion adults who were pregnant
- NC Health Choice (CHIP) participants
- Existing full Medicaid beneficiaries who were pregnant

Coverage applies regardless of immigration status categories eligible for Medicaid. Beneficiaries who gave birth between **February 1, 2022 and March 31, 2022** also received retroactive extension to the full 12 months.

### Continuous Eligibility Protection

A key feature: beneficiaries **maintain postpartum coverage even if circumstances change**, including:
- Income changes (above or below eligibility thresholds)
- Changes in household/family composition
- Loss of employment
- Other changes that would normally trigger redetermination

Coverage lasts through the **last day of the month 12 months after the last date of the pregnancy** (regardless of outcome — live birth, miscarriage, stillbirth, or termination).

## Coverage Scope

The 12-month postpartum period provides access to NC Medicaid's **full benefit package**, which includes:

| Service Category | Included |
|---|---|
| Primary/preventive care | Yes |
| Prescription drugs | Yes |
| Dental care | Yes (adult dental added Dec 2023) |
| Vision care | Yes |
| Behavioral health services | Yes |
| Substance use disorder treatment | Yes |
| Obstetric follow-up | Yes |
| Contraception | Yes |
| Chronic disease management | Yes |

### Behavioral Health Coverage During Postpartum Period

Postpartum mental health is a critical component. Under NC Medicaid:

- **Postpartum depression (PPD) screening**: Obstetric, family practice, and pediatric providers may bill for up to **3 brief emotional/behavioral assessments** (with scoring and documentation) per standardized instrument during the first year after delivery
- **Telehealth**: NC Medicaid temporarily (during COVID-19) and then permanently allowed postpartum depression screenings via remote modalities
- Full behavioral health services — including outpatient therapy, partial hospitalization, intensive outpatient, and medication management — are covered during the extended postpartum period
- SUD treatment: Full ASAM-level SUD services are available, including MAT (buprenorphine, XR-naltrexone, methadone through OTPs)

**Research context:** Perinatal NC Medicaid beneficiaries showed significant behavioral health needs; postpartum mental health disorders affect approximately 1 in 5 birthing people. The extension is designed in part to bridge the gap between postpartum follow-up (traditionally at 6 weeks) and the full first year, during which mood disorders, SUD relapse, and medical complications remain elevated.

## Implementation Under NC Medicaid Managed Care

The postpartum extension operates across all NC Medicaid delivery systems:

- **Standard Plans**: Health plans (Ambetter/Centene, Blue Cross NC, United HealthCare, WellCare) must honor postpartum coverage for enrolled members
- **Tailored Plans**: BH I/DD Tailored Plans (Alliance, Partners, Trillium, Vaya) cover postpartum members with complex behavioral health and I/DD needs
- **NC Medicaid Direct (FFS)**: Covers postpartum members not in managed care (e.g., tribal members in EBCI Tribal Option, certain dual eligibles)

Plans must implement eligibility protections and cannot terminate postpartum coverage for members who experience qualifying changes during the 12-month window.

## Scale and Impact

CMS estimated that up to **28,000 additional people** in NC would become eligible for Medicaid or CHIP for a full year after pregnancy under this extension. North Carolina's maternal mortality rate — disproportionately affecting Black birthing people — makes the postpartum extension a significant health equity intervention.

NC had approximately **100,000–120,000 Medicaid-paid deliveries per year** at the time of implementation (NC Medicaid covers approximately 50%+ of all NC births), making this one of the larger postpartum coverage programs in the southeast.

# NC Medicaid Telehealth Policy

## Overview

North Carolina Medicaid provides comprehensive telehealth coverage through **Clinical Coverage Policy (CCP) 1H — Telehealth, Virtual Communications, and Remote Patient Monitoring**. NC is among **32 state Medicaid programs** that reimburse for all four recognized telehealth modalities.

## Four Covered Telehealth Modalities

| Modality | Description | NC Medicaid Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| **Live video (synchronous)** | Real-time two-way video between patient and provider | Yes — primary modality |
| **Store-and-forward (asynchronous)** | Recorded images/data sent to specialist for review | Yes — with limitations by specialty |
| **Remote patient monitoring (RPM)** | Continuous/periodic digital health data collection (blood pressure, glucose, etc.) | Yes |
| **Audio-only (synchronous)** | Telephone-only visits (no video) | Yes — for patients without video access |

## January 2025 CPT Code Changes

Significant telehealth coding changes effective **January 1, 2025**:

### New AMA Telehealth Codes
- **CPT 98000–98007**: New telehealth codes without in-person equivalent (new AMA codes)
- **CPT 98008–98016**: Synchronous audio-only codes — replaced previous telephonic E/M codes (99441–99443, G2012)
- NC was among the first states to incorporate the new AMA telehealth codes

### NC-Specific Coding Notes
- Providers must use updated codes for audio-only visits starting January 1, 2025
- Legacy codes (99441–99443, G2012) are no longer valid for audio-only after transition date
- Out-of-state providers may bill telehealth for NC Medicaid members IF actively enrolled as NC Medicaid providers

## Provider Requirements

### To bill NC Medicaid telehealth:
1. Be **actively enrolled** in NC Medicaid (NCTRACKS enrollment required for all providers, including out-of-state)
2. Be a covered provider type under CCP 1H
3. Use appropriate place-of-service (POS) and modifier codes
4. Document that telehealth was clinically appropriate

### Eligible Provider Types (examples)
- Physicians (MD/DO)
- Licensed clinical social workers (LCSW)
- Licensed professional counselors (LPC)
- Licensed marriage and family therapists (LMFT)
- Nurse practitioners (NP) and physician assistants (PA)
- Psychologists
- FQHCs (telehealth delivered at the FQHC site)

## Key Policy Provisions (CCP 1H)

### Patient Location
- Patients may receive telehealth from **any location** (home, school, FQHC, community setting)
- No requirement to be in a rural/Health Professional Shortage Area for most services (post-COVID policy retained)

### Audio-Only Limitations
- Audio-only covered but subject to clinical appropriateness
- BH services: audio-only more broadly covered (access equity rationale)
- Some physical health exam-dependent services may require video or in-person

### Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM)
- Covered for chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, COPD, etc.)
- Requires physician order
- 30-day monitoring cycles

### Store-and-Forward
- Covered for dermatology, ophthalmology, radiology interpretation
- Asynchronous — no real-time patient involvement

## Equity Implications

The Center for Health Care Strategies (CHCS) and Milbank Memorial Fund conducted research on NC Medicaid telehealth equity:
- **Digital divide** is a barrier in rural NC and for older adults, low-income populations
- Audio-only coverage is critical equity mechanism — not all Medicaid members have broadband or video-capable devices
- NC Medicaid's expansion of audio-only (maintained from COVID flexibilities) has been associated with more equitable access
- Black and rural Medicaid members showed different telehealth utilization patterns by modality

## Behavioral Health Telehealth

BH services are the most telehealth-intensive in NC Medicaid:
- Therapy (individual and group) widely delivered via live video
- Psychiatric medication management via video
- SUD counseling (individual and group) via telehealth
- Crisis services: telehealth consultation supplements, but mobile crisis teams remain in-person
- OTP/methadone: federal rules allow take-home flexibilities and audio-only counseling (post-COVID rules extended)

## Standard Plan and Tailored Plan Telehealth

- **Standard Plans and Tailored Plans** must cover telehealth in accordance with CCP 1H
- Plans cannot impose more restrictive requirements than NC Medicaid Direct without prior approval
- Plans may negotiate telehealth rates with providers

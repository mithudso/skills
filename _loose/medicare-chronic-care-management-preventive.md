# Medicare Preventive Services & Care Management

Medicare covers a broad set of preventive and care coordination benefits — many at **$0 cost-sharing** when provided by a participating provider. Understanding these benefits helps beneficiaries maximize coverage.

## The Annual Wellness Visit (AWV)

**What it is:** A yearly health review — NOT a comprehensive physical examination. Focuses on health history, risk factors, creating a personalized prevention plan, and screening schedules.

**Cost:** $0 (no deductible, no coinsurance) when provided by a Medicare-participating provider

| AWV Type | CPT Code | When Covered |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| Initial AWV (first AWV ever) | G0438 | Once per lifetime |
| Subsequent AWV | G0439 | Once every 12 months after initial |
| Initial Preventive Physical Exam (IPPE/"Welcome to Medicare") | G0402 | Once in first 12 months of Part B |

**What the AWV includes:**
- Health risk assessment (HRA) — questionnaire completed by patient
- Review of medical history and current providers/medications
- Cognitive impairment assessment (Mini-Cog or similar)
- Depression screening
- Functional ability and safety screening
- Blood pressure measurement
- Height, weight, BMI
- Schedule/referrals for other appropriate preventive services
- Advance care planning discussion (can be added to AWV without extra copay)

**What the AWV does NOT include:**
- Full hands-on physical examination
- Diagnosis or treatment of existing conditions
- Lab tests (these can be ordered but are billed separately and may have cost-sharing)

**Key rule:** If the provider addresses a new or existing medical problem during the same visit, that portion may be billed as a separate E/M service — which could trigger cost-sharing (20% coinsurance). Ask your provider to keep the AWV separate or to defer problem-based care to another visit.

## The Welcome to Medicare Visit (IPPE)

- One-time benefit in first 12 months of Part B enrollment
- Similar to AWV but specifically designed for new enrollees
- $0 cost-sharing
- Establishes baseline for future preventive planning

## Chronic Care Management (CCM)

**Who qualifies:** Medicare beneficiaries with **two or more chronic conditions** expected to last at least 12 months or until death — such as diabetes + hypertension, COPD + heart failure, etc.

**What it covers (when billed by eligible providers):**
- At least 20 minutes of non-face-to-face clinical staff time per month, directed by a physician or qualified practitioner
- 24/7 care coordination access
- Personalized care plan accessible electronically
- Medication management
- Coordination with other care providers
- Transitional care activities

| Code | Service | Time |
|------|---------|------|
| 99490 | Standard CCM | ≥20 min/month |
| 99491 | Complex CCM (physician time) | ≥30 min/month |
| 99487 | Complex CCM (high complexity) | ≥60 min/month |

**Cost to beneficiary:** 80% covered after annual deductible (20% coinsurance applies); MSP or Medigap may cover the remainder.

**Initiating visit requirement:** Before CCM services begin, beneficiary must have had a face-to-face visit with the billing provider within the past year (or at an AWV or IPPE).

**Consent:** Beneficiary must provide verbal or written consent to CCM services and can revoke at any time.

**Who can bill:** Physicians, nurse practitioners, physician assistants, clinical nurse specialists, and certified nurse midwives.

## Transitional Care Management (TCM)

Covers care coordination within 30 days of discharge from inpatient hospital, SNF, or community mental health center:

| Code | Service | Within |
|------|---------|--------|
| 99495 (moderate) | Interactive contact within 2 business days + face-to-face within 14 days | 30 days post-discharge |
| 99496 (high) | Interactive contact within 2 business days + face-to-face within 7 days | 30 days post-discharge |

Cost: 20% coinsurance after deductible; Medigap covers remainder.

## Medicare Preventive Services Catalog (2026)

**$0 cost-sharing screenings and services (when medically appropriate):**

| Service | Frequency | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Annual Wellness Visit | Annually | No cost-sharing |
| Mammography (screening) | Annually after age 40 | $0 for screening only; diagnostic has cost-sharing |
| Colorectal cancer screening | Colonoscopy every 10 yr (high risk: 2 yr); annual stool test | $0 for screening colonoscopy; if polyp found and removed, cost-sharing may apply |
| Bone density (DEXA) | Every 2 years (osteoporosis risk) | $0 |
| Diabetes prevention program (CDC-recognized) | Up to 2 years of sessions | $0 (eligible if at-risk) |
| Lung cancer CT screening | Annually (age 50–77, 20 pack-yr history, current/quit <15 yr) | $0 |
| Cardiovascular disease screening | Every 5 years | $0 (lipids) |
| Depression screening | Annually | $0 |
| Alcohol misuse screening and counseling | Annually (+ 4 brief counseling sessions if misuse detected) | $0 |
| Obesity screening/counseling | Annually + intensive behavioral therapy | $0 |
| Flu vaccine | Annually | $0 |
| Pneumococcal vaccine (PPSV23, PCV15, PCV20) | Per schedule | $0 |
| COVID-19 vaccine | Per schedule | $0 |
| Hepatitis B vaccine | Per schedule | $0 |
| Abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA) screening | Once (male, smoked 100+ cigarettes, age 65–75) | $0 |
| Cervical/vaginal cancer screening (Pap/HPV) | Every 24 months (every 12 months if high risk) | $0 |
| Glaucoma screening | Annually (high risk: diabetics, family history, African American 50+) | $0 |

**Note:** The $0 cost-sharing applies when the PRIMARY purpose of the visit is the preventive service. If a physician addresses other conditions in the same visit, regular cost-sharing may apply to that portion.

## Practical Tips for Beneficiaries

1. **Schedule your AWV separately** from other appointments — asking about a "new symptom" during AWV may trigger a billable office visit
2. **Ask your PCP about CCM** if you have multiple chronic conditions — many practices offer it and it costs you little under Medigap
3. **Colorectal screening trap:** If a screening colonoscopy finds and removes a polyp, Medicare may reclassify it as diagnostic — coinsurance applies. The No Surprises Act did not fully fix this; confirm with your provider
4. **Lung cancer screening:** Requires shared decision-making conversation with physician and order — it's not automatic
5. **Keep your Medicare Summary Notice (MSN):** Review to catch billing errors on preventive services

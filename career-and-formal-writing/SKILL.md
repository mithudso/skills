# Career, Academic, Legal & Formal Writing

Genres here share one trait: governed by **external conventions you don't invent**. Resume judged against recruiter/ATS expectations; citation right or wrong by published style manual; privacy notice read by counsel and regulator; policy audited; survey question measured for bias. Craft here less about voice, more about meeting fixed standard precisely — still reading human.

Hub covers eight domains: resumes/CVs, cover letters, job descriptions, performance reviews, academic/citation writing, legal-adjacent drafting, policy/governance writing, survey question design.

## How this hub works

This SKILL.md **consolidates 8 career/formal sub-skills as on-demand references**.
Routing table maps task to reference file. When task matches row, **Read listed `references/<name>.md` before producing deep answer or draft** — reference carries frameworks, templates, formulas, anti-patterns this overview only names. Don't answer from table alone for anything beyond quick pointer.

Legal-adjacent and policy/governance writing carry hard rule: draft text for review by qualified counsel or governance owner, not substitute. Flag that boundary in any output touching those domains.

# Modafinil and Armodafinil Pharmacology

## Core Concepts
- Modafinil (Provigil) is a racemic 1:1 mixture of R- and S-enantiomers; armodafinil (Nuvigil) is the purified R-enantiomer
- Both are Schedule IV wakefulness-promoting agents FDA-approved for narcolepsy, OSA-related EDS, and shift-work disorder
- Mechanistically distinct from amphetamines: no significant catecholamine release, lower abuse potential, no peripheral sympathomimetic excess
- Mechanism debate unresolved: originally proposed as non-specific (histamine/orexin/norepinephrine), now strong evidence for dopamine transporter (DAT) inhibition as primary driver
- CYP3A4 inducer with clinically significant drug interaction profile

## Mechanisms / Biology
- **DAT inhibition (primary):** Modafinil and armodafinil bind DAT and inhibit dopamine reuptake; PET studies in humans confirm striatal dopamine elevation at therapeutic doses — DAT occupancy required for wakefulness effects
- **Atypical DAT binding:** Binds at the same site as cocaine but induces an "outward-facing" DAT conformation; slower association/dissociation kinetics = lower abuse liability and reduced reinforcement vs. classical stimulants
- **R-enantiomer potency:** R-modafinil has ~3× higher DAT affinity than S-modafinil; the S-enantiomer is rapidly cleared (t½ ~4 h vs. ~15 h for R), making armodafinil pharmacokinetically superior for sustained wakefulness
- **Secondary systems:** Inhibits norepinephrine reuptake (NET); activates orexin/hypocretin neurons and histaminergic tuberomammillary nucleus neurons downstream of DAT effect — these pathways amplify arousal but do not appear to be primary targets
- **GABA/glutamate modulation:** Modafinil decreases GABA release and increases glutamate in wake-active regions; functional role uncertain but consistent across studies
- **CYP3A4 induction mechanism:** Modafinil induces hepatic and intestinal CYP3A4 via pregnane X receptor (PXR) activation; armodafinil shares this property at therapeutic doses

## Clinical Picture
- First-line pharmacotherapy for excessive daytime sleepiness (EDS) in narcolepsy type 1 and type 2 per AASM guidelines; widely preferred over amphetamines due to tolerability
- Modafinil dose: 200 mg/day once in the morning (standard); up to 400 mg/day tolerated but without consistent additional efficacy evidence
- Armodafinil dose: 150–250 mg/day; later plasma Tmax (~4 h vs. 2–4 h for modafinil) produces more sustained afternoon wakefulness compared to modafinil's earlier peak
- Armodafinil 150 mg achieves similar or greater late-day alertness vs. modafinil 200 mg in head-to-head PK models due to enantiomeric pharmacokinetics
- EDS response rate: ~70–80% of narcolepsy patients achieve clinically meaningful improvement; does not treat cataplexy

## Evidence & Treatment
- **Randomized trials:** Multiple double-blind RCTs (Nuvigil NDA package, US Modafinil in Narcolepsy Multicenter Study Group) demonstrate significant improvement on Maintenance of Wakefulness Test (MWT) and Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS) vs. placebo
- **Drug interactions (CYP3A4 induction):** Reduces systemic exposure of CYP3A4 substrates — ethinyl estradiol AUC decreased ~18%, cyclosporine ~50%; clinically significant for hormonal contraceptives (backup contraception required during and ≥1 month after), immunosuppressants, and some antiretrovirals
- **CYP2C19 inhibition:** Modafinil weakly inhibits CYP2C19 → may increase levels of omeprazole, phenytoin, diazepam; monitor narrow therapeutic index drugs
- **Tolerability:** Headache (~34%), nausea (~11%), anxiety, insomnia common; rare serious: SJS/TEN, angioedema, multi-organ hypersensitivity (black box consideration); cardiovascular effects minimal vs. amphetamines
- **Pregnancy/lactation:** Category C; avoid unless benefit clearly outweighs risk; excreted in breast milk in animal models

## Key Facts
- Modafinil t½: ~15 h (multiple dose, R-enantiomer dominant); armodafinil t½: ~15 h as single enantiomer
- R-modafinil constitutes >90% of circulating modafinil at steady state due to rapid S-enantiomer clearance
- DAT occupancy studies (PET): modafinil 200–400 mg achieves ~50–70% DAT occupancy in striatum — comparable to methylphenidate's therapeutic range
- Oral bioavailability: ~100% for both agents; food delays Tmax but does not affect total absorption
- Ethinyl estradiol Cmax reduced 11% and AUC reduced 18% with modafinil 400 mg/day co-administration; failure risk for low-dose OCP users

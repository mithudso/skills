# Low-Sodium Oxybate Formulation

## Core Concepts
- **Xywav** (calcium, magnesium, potassium, and sodium oxybates; JZP-258) is the low-sodium oxybate formulation approved by FDA, containing the same active moiety (gamma-hydroxybutyrate, GHB) as high-sodium Xyrem
- Contains **92% less sodium** than sodium oxybate (Xyrem): 131 mg sodium per 9 g nightly dose vs. 1,640 mg in Xyrem — a reduction of ~1,500 mg/day at maximum dose
- Salt cations (calcium, magnesium, potassium) replace most sodium while preserving bioequivalent GHB delivery and pharmacokinetic profile
- FDA-approved July 2020 for cataplexy/EDS in narcolepsy (ages ≥7 years); August 2021 for idiopathic hypersomnia (IH) in adults — the first drug ever approved for IH
- REMS-restricted (Jazz Pharmaceuticals); dispensed through a single certified pharmacy network due to CNS depressant and abuse-potential risks

## Mechanisms / Biology
- Active moiety GHB binds GABA-B receptors and GHB receptors to consolidate nocturnal sleep, increase slow-wave sleep (SWS stages 3/4), reduce SOREMP frequency, and suppress cataplexy
- Low-sodium formulation does **not** alter CNS pharmacodynamics — the therapeutic mechanism is unchanged; only the counterion load is reduced
- High dietary sodium elevates blood pressure via plasma volume expansion and sympathetic activation; removing ~1,500 mg/day of sodium is comparable in antihypertensive effect to a modest pharmacologic agent
- Patients with narcolepsy carry elevated baseline cardiovascular and cardiometabolic risk relative to the general population, amplifying the clinical significance of excess sodium exposure
- FDA formally recognized the 92% sodium reduction as **clinically meaningful for reducing cardiovascular morbidity** in the narcolepsy population — an explicit regulatory acknowledgment embedded in the approval rationale

## Clinical Picture
- Xywav improves cataplexy frequency and EDS in narcolepsy type 1 and type 2; for IH, reduces Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS) scores, Patient Global Impression of Change (PGIC), and IH Severity Scale scores vs. placebo (all p < 0.0001) in the pivotal Phase 3 randomized-withdrawal trial
- SEGUE study (NCT04794491): open-label interventional switch study demonstrating that patients stable on Xyrem can transition to gram-for-gram equivalent Xywav doses safely and with maintained efficacy
- Blood pressure reduction data: switching to Xywav produces clinically significant systolic BP reductions in narcolepsy patients with hypertension or cardiometabolic risk factors
- Oxford Academic SLEEP 2024 analysis confirmed LXB efficacy was preserved in narcolepsy patients with and without cardiovascular/cardiometabolic comorbidities
- Approved pediatric population ≥7 years for narcolepsy; IH approval is adults only

## Evidence & Treatment
- Two pivotal Phase 3 randomized-withdrawal, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials: one in narcolepsy (cataplexy and EDS endpoints), one in IH (ESS primary endpoint) — both demonstrated statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvements
- Gram-for-gram dose equivalence with Xyrem permits direct 1:1 switch without re-titration; SEGUE study validated this transition approach
- Preferred over Xyrem in patients with: hypertension, heart failure, CKD, sodium-restricted diets, or any elevated cardiovascular risk — the FDA label explicitly references this benefit
- Also formulated as **Lumryz** (once-nightly extended-release sodium oxybate), but Lumryz retains high sodium — Xywav remains the only low-sodium oxybate option
- Administered as two nightly doses (at bedtime and 2.5–4 hours later); once-nightly formulation research is ongoing

## Key Facts
- **131 mg sodium** per maximum 9 g nightly Xywav dose vs. **1,640 mg** in equivalent Xyrem dose
- **92%** sodium reduction; equivalent to removing ~1,500 mg sodium/day from diet
- FDA approval dates: narcolepsy **July 2020**; idiopathic hypersomnia **August 2021** (first-ever IH approval)
- Approved age range: **≥7 years** (narcolepsy); **adults only** (IH)
- Dose range: 4.5–9 g/night split into two doses; titrated from 4.5 g over weeks

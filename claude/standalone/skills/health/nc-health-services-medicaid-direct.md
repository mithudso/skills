# NC Medicaid Direct (Fee-for-Service)

## Overview

**NC Medicaid Direct** is North Carolina's traditional fee-for-service (FFS) Medicaid delivery system, operating alongside the managed care system. While most NC Medicaid members are enrolled in **Standard Plans** or **Tailored Plans**, specific populations remain in Medicaid Direct.

**Administrative system**: NC Medicaid Direct claims and eligibility are processed through **NCTRACKS**, the NC Medicaid Management Information System.

**Website**: [ncmedicaidplans.gov — NC Medicaid Direct services](https://ncmedicaidplans.gov/en/nc-medicaid-direct-services)

## Who Is in NC Medicaid Direct?

Populations typically remaining in FFS / Medicaid Direct include:

| Population | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Medicare-Medicaid dual eligibles** (full duals) | Medicare is primary; Medicaid wraps cost-sharing. Some duals in managed care for BH services (Tailored Plans) |
| **American Indian/Alaska Native (AIAN) beneficiaries** | Federal law: American Indians have the right to opt out of managed care and receive FFS |
| **Medically needy / spend-down** | Individuals who qualify by spending down to Medicaid income threshold |
| **Certain LTSS populations** | Those receiving CAP/DA, CAP/C, Personal Care Services outside managed care |
| **Foster care / former foster youth** | Certain cohorts pending CFSP launch (planned Dec 2025) |
| **Short-enrollment gap categories** | Individuals between managed care plan assignments |
| **Retroactive eligibility** | Claims processed retroactively before managed care enrollment |

## Services Covered Under Medicaid Direct

NC Medicaid Direct covers the full NC Medicaid State Plan benefit package:
- Physician services
- Hospital (inpatient and outpatient)
- FQHC and Rural Health Clinic services (at PPS-APM rate)
- Dental services (same adult benefit as managed care)
- Vision and hearing
- Pharmacy (same formulary as managed care)
- Mental health and SUD services
- Home health
- Personal Care Services (PCS)
- Long-term care (nursing facilities, ICFs)
- Durable medical equipment (DME)

## Reimbursement Under Medicaid Direct

### Fee Schedules
NC Medicaid publishes detailed fee schedules at the [NC Medicaid Fee Schedule portal](https://ncdhhs.servicenowservices.com/fee_schedules):
- Updated periodically (quarterly or annually)
- Procedure code-based (CPT, HCPCS)
- Separate schedules for different provider types

### FFS Rate Reductions (2025 Context)
NC Medicaid faced budget pressures in 2025; **rate reduction** proposals were announced and subsequently subject to partial reversal (see NC Medicaid Rate Reduction Reversal Update, December 2025).

### FQHC/RHC in Medicaid Direct
- New PPS-APM reimbursement operationalized **May 1, 2024** for Medicaid Direct
- Provider-specific rate based on 113% of each FQHC's 2021 Medicaid costs

### CAP PCS Rate Updates
- Personal Care Services rates updated February 2024 (CAP/DA, CAP/C, CAP/CD correction)
- Congregate settings PCS methodology updated December 2024

## NCTRACKS

**NCTRACKS** is the NC Medicaid claims and eligibility system used for Medicaid Direct billing:
- Provider enrollment
- Claims submission and adjudication
- Prior authorization
- Eligibility verification
- Remittance advice
- Fee schedule downloads

All providers billing Medicaid Direct must be enrolled in NCTRACKS. Managed care providers have additional enrollment requirements with each plan.

## Relationship to Managed Care

NC Medicaid Direct exists alongside managed care — not as a remnant but as an intentional ongoing pathway for populations that:
1. Have a legal right to FFS (American Indians)
2. Have complex insurance coordination needs (duals)
3. Are in transition between coverage categories

The NC Medicaid "dual track" system (Direct + managed care) requires coordination to prevent coverage gaps and duplicate billing.

# NC Rural Health Programs and Provider Shortages

## Overview

North Carolina's rural health crisis is significant — 74 of 100 counties lack adequate primary care providers, and mental health access in rural areas can reach ratios of 5,000 patients per provider. Multiple programs address rural health access, culminating in a **$213 million federal Rural Health Transformation Program award** in December 2025.

## NC Office of Rural Health (ORH)

**Part of NCDHHS**, the Office of Rural Health:
- Designates **Health Professional Shortage Areas (HPSAs)** and Medically Underserved Areas/Populations (MUA/MUPs) in NC
- Administers **provider recruitment and placement** programs (loan repayment, J-1 visa waivers, placement matching)
- Funds rural health programs through grants
- Coordinates the Rural Health Transformation Program
- Produces maps of HPSA-designated counties ([HPSA designations](https://www.ncdhhs.gov/allhpsa/download))

## Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP)

**Award**: North Carolina received **$213 million** from CMS through the Rural Health Transformation Program — announced December 29, 2025.

### Program Scope
- 5-year federal investment
- Will improve health care delivery for **3+ million rural North Carolinians**
- Supports **400+ rural health facilities**
- Strategic roadmap to improve rural health infrastructure

### Background
- NC applied for $200M in November 2025
- CMS awarded $213M in December 2025
- Part of CMS's national rural health transformation initiative

### Focus Areas (anticipated)
- Rural hospital sustainability
- Workforce recruitment and retention
- Telehealth expansion in rural communities
- Primary care and specialty access
- Behavioral health access

## Provider Shortage Scale

### Primary Care
- **74 of 100 NC counties** have a shortage of primary care providers (>1,500 residents per provider)
- Only **1–3% of graduates** from NC medical schools end up practicing in rural areas
- Rural hospitals face escalating staffing costs: FirstHealth reported 243 vacant RN positions out of 1,544 (15.7% vacancy)

### Mental Health (Rural)
- Patient-to-mental-health-provider ratio in some rural NC areas: **5,000:1**
- Mental health professionals frequently do not accept Medicaid/Medicare (the dominant payers for rural populations)
- WECT (Jan 2026): "North Carolina faces severe mental health provider shortage in rural areas"
- NC lawmakers raised concerns at the Capitol over rural staffing crisis (April 2026)

## Provider Recruitment Programs (NC ORH)

**State Loan Repayment Program (SLRP)**: Loan repayment for primary care physicians, dentists, and mental health professionals who commit to 2+ years in an HPSA.

**J-1 Visa Waiver Program**: Allows international medical graduates on J-1 visas to remain in the US by serving in underserved areas.

**Provider Matching**: ORH maintains a database of practices seeking providers in rural/underserved areas.

## Rural Hospitals and Federal Funding Threats

NC's rural hospitals are particularly vulnerable:
- Most are Critical Access Hospitals (CAH) — small hospitals (≤25 beds) receiving enhanced Medicare reimbursement
- CAHs rely heavily on Medicare and Medicaid (rural populations disproportionately covered)
- Federal cuts to Medicare/Medicaid reimbursement threaten CAH financial viability
- NC lawmakers (2026 legislative session) raising concerns about federal funding cuts to rural hospitals

## Medicaid Expansion and Rural NC

NC Medicaid Expansion (December 2023) had outsized impact on rural communities:
- **244,500+ rural residents** enrolled — more than 1 in 3 of all newly eligible people
- Rural areas had among the highest uninsured rates pre-expansion
- However, provider shortages mean coverage doesn't automatically translate to access

## Rural Health Clinics (RHCs)

RHCs are federally certified clinics in rural shortage areas:
- Receive cost-based Medicaid reimbursement (PPS-APM, same framework as FQHCs)
- May be provider-based (hospital-affiliated) or independent
- New APM reimbursement effective August 2024 and May 2024 (Medicaid Direct)
- Critical access points where FQHCs are not present

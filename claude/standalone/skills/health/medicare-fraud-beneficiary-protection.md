# Medicare Fraud, Waste & Abuse: Beneficiary Protection

Medicare fraud costs the program billions annually — and directly harms beneficiaries through unnecessary procedures, identity theft, and misleading plan marketing. Every beneficiary should know the common schemes and reporting channels.

## Common Fraud Schemes Targeting Beneficiaries

### 1. Provider Billing Fraud
- **Phantom billing:** Charging for services never delivered (most common type)
- **Upcoding:** Billing for a more expensive procedure or service than was actually provided
- **Unbundling:** Separating services that should be billed together to increase payment
- **Double-billing:** Submitting the same claim to Medicare multiple times
- **Medically unnecessary services:** Ordering tests or treatments a patient doesn't need

### 2. DME (Durable Medical Equipment) Fraud
Scammers target beneficiaries with unsolicited offers of "free" wheelchairs, back braces, knee braces, or other equipment. The equipment may never be delivered — or is delivered unnecessarily — while Medicare is billed. Signs:
- Calls or door-to-door offers of free equipment
- Someone asking for your Medicare number to "process your free equipment"
- Equipment arrives that you never requested

### 3. Genetic Testing Scams
Common at health fairs, community events, pharmacies:
- Offer "free" cancer screening, genetic test, or pharmacogenomics testing
- Collect your Medicare number and cheek swab
- Submit inflated claims to Medicare for tests you may not have needed
- Results may never be provided or acted upon

**Medicare rule:** Genetic tests must be ordered by your treating physician based on a medical need — not marketed at events.

### 4. Identity and Medicare Card Scams
- Calls claiming "Medicare" or "Social Security" need to verify your Medicare number to update your card or prevent benefit suspension — **Medicare never calls you to ask for your Medicare number**
- Offers to send a "new Medicare card" requiring you to read your number back
- Data breach impersonation calls

**Protect your Medicare number:** Treat it like a credit card number. Only give it to: your doctor, pharmacist, other trusted healthcare providers, your insurer, or people you've contacted at Medicare.

### 5. Open Enrollment Scams
During AEP (Oct 15–Dec 7), scammers impersonate Medicare to push plan switches:
- Unsolicited calls claiming your current plan is being discontinued
- Pressure to switch Medicare Advantage plans immediately
- Misrepresentation of plan benefits

**Medicare rule:** Licensed agents cannot make unsolicited calls to enroll you in a Medicare Advantage or Part D plan unless you've asked them to contact you.

## Medicare Advantage Fraud & Marketing Abuses

CMS has increasingly scrutinized MA plan marketing:

### Ghost networks
Provider directories that list physicians or facilities that are not actually in-network — or no longer accepting new MA patients. If you relied on a directory to choose a plan and providers are unavailable, this may constitute misleading information.
- **If this happens:** File a grievance with the plan, and report to your State Insurance Commissioner and CMS

### Misleading supplemental benefit advertising
MA plans advertise extras (dental, vision, hearing, fitness, meals) that may be more limited than advertised:
- Benefits may require prior authorization or have low annual dollar limits
- "Dental coverage" might cover only cleanings, not major work
- 2026 CMS change: MA provider directories now integrated into Medicare Plan Finder for easier verification

### Agent misrepresentation
Licensed agents must comply with CMS marketing rules. Common violations:
- Claiming a plan covers something it doesn't
- Not disclosing plan limitations
- Enrolling you in a plan without your full understanding
- Using a free meal seminar to pressure enrollment

## How to Detect Fraud in Your Own Benefits

### Read Your Medicare Summary Notice (MSN)
The MSN arrives quarterly (for Original Medicare) and lists all claims submitted on your behalf:
1. Check dates of service — were you actually seen on those dates?
2. Check procedures — were those the services you received?
3. Check providers — do you recognize all the names?
4. Check amounts — does the billed amount match what you were told?

**If you find an error:** First contact the provider (may be a billing mistake). If not resolved, call 1-800-MEDICARE or contact your SMP.

### Medicare Advantage EOB (Explanation of Benefits)
MA plans must send an EOB monthly (when you had a claim). Same review process applies.

## Reporting Fraud and Getting Help

### Reporting channels

| Channel | Contact | Use For |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 1-800-MEDICARE | 1-800-633-4227 | Billing questions, MSN errors, first-line reports |
| OIG Hotline | 1-800-HHS-TIPS (1-800-447-8477) | Formal fraud reporting; anonymous available |
| OIG Online | oig.hhs.gov/fraud/report-fraud | Online fraud tip submission |
| Senior Medicare Patrol (SMP) | smpresource.org | Free counseling; help reviewing MSN and reporting |
| FTC | 1-877-IDTHEFT | Medicare identity theft specifically |
| State Insurance Commissioner | Varies by state | MA plan marketing violations |

### Senior Medicare Patrol (SMP)
SMPs are federally funded programs in every state that help beneficiaries:
- Understand Medicare bills
- Detect potential fraud, errors, and abuse
- Report suspected fraud to appropriate agencies
- Free, confidential, non-judgmental help

### SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program)
Free one-on-one counseling for Medicare questions — including evaluating whether a plan change is appropriate and identifying potential marketing violations. Find your state SHIP at: shiphelp.org

## 2026 CMS Enforcement Priorities

CMS and OIG have signaled increased scrutiny on:
- **MA upcoding:** MA plans submitting inflated risk-adjustment diagnoses to increase payments
- **MA marketing abuses:** Third-party marketing organizations (TPMOs) making misleading claims
- **Genetic testing fraud:** Continued crackdown on unnecessary testing at community events
- **Telehealth fraud:** Post-pandemic growth in fraudulent telehealth billing
- **MA ghost networks:** Integration of provider directories into Medicare Plan Finder to improve verification

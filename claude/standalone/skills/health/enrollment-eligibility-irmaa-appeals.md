# Medicare IRMAA: Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount

## What Is IRMAA?

IRMAA is a surcharge added to the standard Medicare Part B and Part D premiums for beneficiaries with higher incomes. Established by the Medicare Modernization Act (MMA) 2003 and expanded by the ACA, IRMAA applies to individuals with Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) above specified thresholds.

**IRMAA applies to:** Both Part B (medical) and Part D (prescription drug) premiums.

## How IRMAA Is Determined

**Income year used:** SSA uses tax return data from **2 years prior** to the coverage year.
- 2025 premiums → based on 2023 tax return MAGI
- 2024 premiums → based on 2022 tax return MAGI

**MAGI for IRMAA:** Adjusted Gross Income (AGI) from tax return + tax-exempt interest income.

**Notification:** SSA sends an IRMAA determination letter (ANOC — Annual Notice of Change) in November/December for the following year.

## 2025 IRMAA Thresholds and Surcharges

### Part B IRMAA (added to standard ~$185/month premium)

| Individual MAGI | Joint MAGI | Total Monthly Part B Premium |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ $106,000 | ≤ $212,000 | $185.00 (no IRMAA) |
| $106,001 – $133,000 | $212,001 – $266,000 | $259.00 |
| $133,001 – $167,000 | $266,001 – $334,000 | $370.00 |
| $167,001 – $200,000 | $334,001 – $400,000 | $480.90 |
| $200,001 – $499,999 | $400,001 – $749,999 | $591.90 |
| ≥ $500,000 | ≥ $750,000 | $628.90 |
| Married filing separately $106,001 – $394,000 | N/A | $591.90 |
| Married filing separately > $394,000 | N/A | $628.90 |

### Part D IRMAA (added to plan premium)

| Individual MAGI | Joint MAGI | Monthly IRMAA Surcharge |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ $106,000 | ≤ $212,000 | $0 |
| $106,001 – $133,000 | $212,001 – $266,000 | $12.90 |
| $133,001 – $167,000 | $266,001 – $334,000 | $33.30 |
| $167,001 – $200,000 | $334,001 – $400,000 | $53.80 |
| $200,001 – $499,999 | $400,001 – $749,999 | $74.20 |
| ≥ $500,000 | ≥ $750,000 | $81.00 |

## Qualifying Life-Changing Events for IRMAA Appeal

If current income is significantly lower than the 2-year-prior income SSA used, you can request a new determination based on current income by demonstrating a qualifying life-changing event (LCE):

1. **Marriage**
2. **Divorce or annulment**
3. **Death of spouse**
4. **Work stoppage** (retirement, layoff, disability)
5. **Work reduction** (reduction in hours/pay)
6. **Loss of income-producing property** (due to disaster, fraud, or other circumstances beyond control)
7. **Loss of pension income** (pension plan termination or employer reorganization)
8. **Employer settlement payment** (certain one-time payments that inflated prior-year income)

**Note:** A one-time spike in income (e.g., Roth IRA conversion, sale of property) in the base year is not itself a qualifying LCE — the event must be a change in ongoing income circumstances.

## How to Appeal IRMAA

### Form SSA-44 (Medicare Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount — Life-Changing Event)

**Purpose:** Request a new IRMAA determination using current or more recent income due to a qualifying LCE.

**What you need:**
- SSA-44 form (downloadable at ssa.gov)
- Evidence of the LCE (death certificate, layoff letter, retirement documentation, etc.)
- Estimate of current year's MAGI (or actual tax return if available)
- Evidence of current income (pay stubs, pension statements)

**Where to submit:**
- Local SSA office (in person or by mail)
- Cannot submit SSA-44 online (as of 2025)

**Timeline:** SSA typically processes appeals within 30–60 days.

### Non-LCE Reconsideration

If you believe SSA used incorrect tax data (wrong year, wrong income amount), you can request a reconsideration:
- SSA receives IRS data; if IRS data was incorrect, can request correction through IRS
- If filing amended tax return, SSA will update IRMAA after IRS processes amended return

## Strategies to Manage IRMAA

1. **Roth conversion timing:** Large Roth conversions spike income; plan 2 years before Medicare enrollment
2. **Capital gains management:** Time large capital gains realizations to avoid crossing IRMAA tiers
3. **Qualified Charitable Distributions (QCDs):** Reduce AGI by donating RMDs directly from IRA to charity (up to $105,000/year in 2025); reduces MAGI
4. **Tax bracket harvesting:** Keep income in lower tiers strategically
5. **Appeal proactively:** If income drops significantly (retirement, spouse death), file SSA-44 immediately — do not wait for next year's determination

## Interaction with Extra Help / LIS

Dual-eligible individuals and those with Extra Help (Low-Income Subsidy) for Part D are NOT subject to IRMAA on Part D — Extra Help overrides IRMAA.

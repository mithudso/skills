# Medicare Enrollment Periods

## Initial Enrollment Period (IEP)

**Who:** Anyone becoming Medicare-eligible for the first time (turning 65 or acquiring 24 months of SSDI).

**Window:** 7-month window:
- 3 months before the month you turn 65
- The month you turn 65
- 3 months after the month you turn 65

**Coverage start:**
- Enroll in months 1–3 (before birthday month) → coverage starts the 1st of your birthday month
- Enroll in birthday month → coverage starts 1st of following month
- Enroll in months 5–7 (after birthday month) → coverage delayed 1–2 months further

**Parts covered:** Part A (hospital), Part B (medical), Part C (Medicare Advantage), Part D (prescription drug).

**Action required:** Part A is usually premium-free and auto-enrolls if already receiving Social Security. Part B, C, D require active enrollment unless auto-enrolled.

## Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) / Open Enrollment Period (OEP)

**AEP:** October 15 – December 7 each year
- Switch between Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage
- Change Medicare Advantage plans
- Add, drop, or change Part D prescription drug plan
- Changes take effect January 1

**Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (MA-OEP):** January 1 – March 31
- Already enrolled in Medicare Advantage: can switch to another MA plan or return to Original Medicare + Part D
- Cannot use to join Medicare Advantage for first time if not already enrolled

## Special Enrollment Periods (SEP)

Qualifying life events that trigger a SEP (bypass late penalties):

| Trigger | SEP Window |
|---|---|
| Lost employer/union group coverage (active worker or dependent) | 8 months after coverage ends or employment ends |
| Move to new service area (for Part C/D) | 2 months around move |
| Involuntary loss of creditable Part D coverage | 2 months |
| LIS/Extra Help status gained or lost | Monthly SEP for Part D |
| Move into/out of institution (nursing home, etc.) | Monthly |
| Contract termination of MA plan | 2 months |
| PACE or cost plan changes | Specific windows |
| 5-star Special Enrollment | December 8 – November 30 (continuous) |

**Most important SEP:** The employer coverage SEP — working past 65 with employer group coverage. Enroll within 8 months of losing that coverage or retiring without penalty.

## General Enrollment Period (GEP)

- January 1 – March 31 each year
- For those who missed IEP and have no SEP
- Coverage starts July 1 (historically) — as of January 2023, coverage starts the month after enrollment (BPCIA fix)
- Late enrollment penalties typically apply

## Part C and Part D Enrollment

**Part C (Medicare Advantage):** Requires first having Parts A and B. Enrollment windows mirror Part B windows. Choose during IEP, AEP, or SEP.

**Part D (Prescription Drug Plan):**
- Enroll during IEP alongside Part B
- If declining Part D initially and have no creditable drug coverage from employer, late penalties apply when enrolling later
- SEP available when losing creditable coverage

## Key Rules

1. **Auto-enrollment:** Those already receiving Social Security at 65 are auto-enrolled in Parts A and B; card mailed ~3 months before 65th birthday.
2. **Premium-free Part A:** Most qualify if they or spouse worked 40+ quarters (10 years) and paid Medicare taxes.
3. **Part B premium:** Standard 2025 premium ~$185/month; IRMAA surcharges for higher incomes.
4. **Declining Part B:** Can opt out during IEP if still covered by employer, but must do so actively or will be auto-enrolled.

# NC Medicaid Managed Care: Standard Plans and Tailored Plans

## Overview

North Carolina Medicaid transitioned from fee-for-service to managed care in two major phases:

- **July 1, 2021**: Standard Plans launched for the general Medicaid population
- **December 1, 2023**: Medicaid Expansion went live (covering adults 19–64 up to 138% FPL, adding ~600,000 enrollees)
- **July 1, 2024**: Behavioral Health and Intellectual/Developmental Disabilities (BH I/DD) Tailored Plans launched
- **December 1, 2025**: Children and Families Specialty Plan (CFSP) scheduled to launch

## Standard Plans

Provide comprehensive physical health services to the general Medicaid population. Managed by private health plans (e.g., Ambetter/Centene, Blue Cross NC, United HealthCare Community Plan, WellCare). Standard Plans cover:

- Primary and specialty care
- Hospital services
- Prescription drugs
- Behavioral health (mild-to-moderate)
- Dental and vision (since Expansion 2023)
- Long-term services and supports (some categories)

FQHC/RHC reimbursed via Alternate Payment Methodology (APM) under Standard Plans as of August 1, 2024.

## Tailored Plans (BH I/DD)

For Medicaid members with **serious mental illness (SMI), severe substance use disorder (SUD), intellectual/developmental disabilities (I/DD), or traumatic brain injury (TBI)**. Approximately 210,000+ North Carolinians enrolled.

**Managed by four LME-MCOs** (not commercial insurance plans):
- Alliance Health
- Partners Health Management
- Trillium Health Resources
- Vaya Health

**What Tailored Plans add** over Standard Plans:
- 1915(c) Innovations and TBI waiver services
- Specialized behavioral health services (ACTT, PACT, Intensive In-Home, etc.)
- I/DD habilitation services
- Enhanced care coordination
- All state-funded enhanced BH and SUD services

**Enrollment**: Members are auto-assigned to a Tailored Plan in their LME-MCO region; choice of Standard Plan not available if meeting eligibility criteria.

## Children and Families Specialty Plan (CFSP)

- Planned launch: December 1, 2025
- For children and families involved with child welfare / foster care
- Will integrate physical health, BH, and social services

## Quality Strategy

The NC Medicaid Managed Care Quality Strategy (2025) sets performance targets across:
- Preventive care rates
- Chronic disease management
- Behavioral health integration
- HEDIS measure performance
- Equity metrics by race/ethnicity/geography

## Medicaid Expansion Impact (Dec 2023)

- 628,681+ adults enrolled by early 2025
- $28.6 million+ in dental claims in first 7 months
- Adults 19–64, ≤138% FPL, not already eligible for full Medicaid
- Includes full benefits: medical, dental, vision, behavioral health

# Competency questions — trading-and-investing family

Routing probes: someone working this subject should be able to ask each question
and reach a HAVE or researched-this-run node. Used by Step 8 coverage saturation
and replayed by skill-tree-architect as routing probes.

1. What's the difference between a call and a put option, and how do the Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega) drive their price? → options-trading-and-strategies
2. How do I size a position and set a stop-loss so a single trade risks at most 1% of my account? → trading-risk-management
3. What does an RSI divergence or a MACD crossover signal on a price chart, and how reliable is it? → technical-analysis
4. How do I backtest a mean-reversion strategy without overfitting or look-ahead bias? → algorithmic-and-quant-trading (+ trading-strategies-and-styles)
5. What is the difference between a market, limit, and stop order, and where does slippage come from? → market-microstructure-and-execution
6. How is a futures contract different from an option, and what are contango and backwardation? → derivatives-futures-and-swaps
7. How do I swap tokens on a DEX, and what is impermanent loss in an AMM liquidity pool? → defi-and-onchain-trading (+ crypto-and-digital-asset-trading)
8. What is the Sharpe ratio, and how does Modern Portfolio Theory pick an efficient portfolio? → portfolio-theory-and-asset-allocation
9. What is the pattern-day-trader (PDT) rule, and how are wash sales and trader taxes handled? → trading-regulation-compliance-and-taxes
10. What macro forces move a currency pair like EUR/USD, and what is the carry trade? → forex-and-currency-trading
11. Why do I keep selling winners early and holding losers — what behavioral bias is that? → trading-psychology-and-behavioral-finance
12. How does a bond's price move with interest rates, and what is duration? → fixed-income-and-bond-markets

# NC Medicaid Behavioral Health and SUD Services

## Overview

NC Medicaid covers a comprehensive array of behavioral health (BH) and substance use disorder (SUD) services through multiple delivery pathways. Major reforms in 2024 brought significant rate increases and new service definitions aligned with ASAM criteria and federal parity requirements.

## Delivery Pathways

### 1. Standard Plans
Cover **mild-to-moderate** BH/SUD services:
- Outpatient therapy (individual, group, family)
- Psychiatric evaluation and medication management
- Outpatient substance abuse services (individual and group)
- Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) for autism
- Community Support Team (moderate level)

### 2. BH I/DD Tailored Plans (launched July 1, 2024)
Cover the full Standard Plan BH/SUD array PLUS **intensive/specialized services**:
- Assertive Community Treatment (ACTT) — for SMI, highest intensity
- Program of Assertive Community Treatment (PACT)
- Intensive In-Home services
- Multisystemic Therapy (MST)
- Functional Family Therapy (FFT)
- Substance Abuse Comprehensive Outpatient Treatment (SACOT)
- Substance Abuse Intensive Outpatient Program (SAIOP)
- Residential services (Level 3.1–3.7 ASAM)
- Medically Managed Intensive Inpatient Withdrawal Management (ASAM Level 4-WM)
- Crisis services (mobile crisis, crisis stabilization)
- All services previously in CCP 8A Enhanced BH and SUD

### 3. NC Medicaid Direct (Fee-for-Service)
Remaining FFS population (dual eligibles, certain LTSS recipients) continues to access BH/SUD through direct billing.

### 4. State-Funded Services (LME-MCOs)
For uninsured/underinsured individuals: LME-MCOs fund basic BH/SUD services with state appropriations.

## 2024 Rate Increases and Policy Changes

### Major Rate Increases (2024)
- **SAIOP and SACOT**: +29.2% effective August 1, 2024
- **BH services rate increases**: Broad increases implemented 2023–2024 to address workforce retention
- **Rate floors**: Implemented October 1, 2024 across all SUD services

### MHPAEA Compliance (Effective January 1, 2025)
NC Medicaid revised Clinical Coverage Policies to comply with the **Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA)**:
- Treatment limitations for BH/SUD services must not be more restrictive than medical/surgical equivalents
- Prior authorization criteria, step therapy, and network adequacy standards reformatted
- Major revision to CCP 8B (Inpatient BH) and CCP 8A (Enhanced BH/SUD Services)

### ASAM Level 4-WM
- **Medically Managed Intensive Inpatient Withdrawal Management** (ASAM Level 4-WM) incorporated into CCP 8B
- Removed from CCP 8A effective January 1, 2025
- Clarifies the pathway for acute alcohol/benzo withdrawal requiring medical monitoring

## Opioid Treatment Programs (OTPs)

NC Medicaid covers **Opioid Treatment Program (OTP)** services under CCP 8A-9:
- Methadone maintenance (daily dispensing)
- Buprenorphine induction and maintenance (office-based)
- Naltrexone extended-release (Vivitrol)
- Counseling (individual, group)
- Drug testing

**2024 statistics**: OTPs served **31,641 individuals** in NC in 2024 (on track to grow in 2025).

**Mobile unit expansion**: Mobile methadone units approved as a place of service (CCP 8A-9 revision), improving rural access.

## Service Tiers (ASAM Framework)

| ASAM Level | Description | NC Medicaid Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | Early Intervention | Yes (outpatient) |
| 1.0 | Outpatient | Yes |
| 2.1 | Intensive Outpatient (IOP/SAIOP) | Yes — +29.2% rate 2024 |
| 2.5 | Partial Hospitalization (SACOT) | Yes — +29.2% rate 2024 |
| 3.1–3.7 | Residential | Yes (Tailored Plans primarily) |
| 4.0 | Medically Managed Inpatient | Yes — CCP 8B |
| 4-WM | Medically Managed Withdrawal | Yes — CCP 8B (2025) |
| OTP | Methadone/MAT clinic | Yes — CCP 8A-9 |

## Key Clinical Coverage Policies (CCPs)

- **CCP 8A**: Enhanced Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services (outpatient, residential, intensive)
- **CCP 8B**: Inpatient Behavioral Health Services (psychiatric, withdrawal management)
- **CCP 8A-9**: Opioid Treatment Programs
- **CCP 8C**: Community Support services
- **CCP 8D**: Residential Treatment for children/adolescents

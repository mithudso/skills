# Medicare Extra Help (Low Income Subsidy / LIS)

Extra Help, also called the Low Income Subsidy (LIS), is a federal program that helps people with limited income and resources pay for Medicare Part D prescription drug costs.

## 2026 Eligibility Thresholds

| Group | Income Limit | Resource Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | $23,475/year (~150% FPL) | $18,090 |
| **Married couple** | $31,725/year | $36,100 |

Resources counted: bank accounts, stocks, bonds, non-primary-home real estate. Resources NOT counted: primary home, one vehicle, life insurance, burial funds up to $1,500.

## Auto-Enrollment (No Application Needed)

CMS automatically enrolls the following individuals in Extra Help (they receive a notice):
- **Full dual eligibles** (enrolled in both Medicare and Medicaid)
- **SSI recipients** (Supplemental Security Income)
- **Medicare Savings Program beneficiaries** (QMB, SLMB, QI)

If auto-enrolled, CMS assigns you to a benchmark Part D plan if you don't choose one. You can switch to any plan at any time (monthly SEP).

## How to Apply (If Not Auto-Enrolled)

Apply through the **Social Security Administration (SSA)**:
1. **Online:** ssa.gov/extrahelp
2. **Phone:** 1-800-772-1213
3. **In person:** Local Social Security office
4. States may also determine LIS eligibility through the Medicaid application process

Applications accepted year-round. Retroactive to the first day of the month you applied if eligible.

## Benefit Levels (2026)

**Full Extra Help (income up to ~135% FPL, or auto-enrolled):**
- Part D premium covered up to the benchmark amount
- **$0 deductible**
- Copays: $5.10 (generics), $12.65 (brand-name) per prescription
- Once you reach the $2,100 out-of-pocket cap: **$0 per prescription** for the rest of the year

**Partial Extra Help (income 135%–150% FPL, applies from application):**
- Sliding scale premium assistance
- Reduced deductible
- Lower copayments than standard Part D

## Extra Help and Part D Late Penalties

If you qualify for Extra Help, **no late enrollment penalty** applies — even if you went months or years without Part D coverage. The penalty is waived for any period you had Extra Help eligibility (even if you didn't know about it).

## Monthly Special Enrollment Period (SEP) for LIS Recipients

Extra Help recipients have a **monthly SEP** — they can switch Part D plans once per month, every month, rather than waiting for AEP. This ensures LIS recipients can always move to the plan that best serves their drug needs.

## Auto-Assignment to Benchmark Plans

When LIS recipients do not choose a Part D plan, CMS assigns them to a **benchmark plan** (plans with premium at or below the regional benchmark premium). If a currently auto-enrolled plan raises its premium above benchmark:
- CMS sends October notice
- Enrollee can select a new plan or be auto-reassigned
- Auto-reassignment ensures no premium obligation for LIS recipients

## Interaction With Medicare Savings Programs

Enrolling in a Medicare Savings Program (QMB, SLMB, QI) automatically triggers Extra Help eligibility. The easiest path to Extra Help for people above LIS income limits but on Medicare Savings Programs is through the MSP application. See `enrollment-eligibility-dual-eligibility-medicare-medicaid` for MSP details.

## Income Definitions for Extra Help

Uses **SSA income counting rules** (not MAGI) — includes:
- Wages, self-employment income
- Social Security and SSI
- Pensions and annuities
- VA benefits (some exclusions)
- Certain exclusions: housing assistance, food stamps, educational grants/scholarships

## Tips for Beneficiaries

- Apply every year — income and resources can change; eligibility may be gained or lost
- Apply even if you're not sure you qualify — SSA will determine
- If denied, ask for reconsideration within 60 days; also ask state Medicaid agency if they can determine eligibility
- SHIP counselors (free) can help with applications — see `enrollment-eligibility-navigator-assister-programs`

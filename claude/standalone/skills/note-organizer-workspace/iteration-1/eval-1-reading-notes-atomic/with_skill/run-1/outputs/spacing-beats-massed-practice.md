# Spacing beats massed practice

Spacing practice out over time works better than massing it, even though spacing *feels* worse. Massed practice ("cramming") produces fast gains that evaporate.

Evidence: Cepeda et al. (meta-analysis) — the optimal spacing gap scales with the retention interval. "Cram for tomorrow, space for forever."

Sources: Make It Stick (Brown et al.); Cepeda et al. (meta-analysis).

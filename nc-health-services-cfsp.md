# NC Children and Families Specialty Plan (CFSP)

## Overview

The **Children and Families Specialty Plan (CFSP)** is NC's newest Medicaid managed care health plan, launched **December 1, 2025**. It is designed specifically for children, youth, and young adults involved with the child welfare system (foster care, adoption, former foster youth).

**Managed by**: **Blue Cross and Blue Shield of North Carolina** under the plan name **Healthy Blue Care Together**

**Approximate enrollment**: ~32,000 beneficiaries at launch

## Who Is Eligible

CFSP serves NC Medicaid-enrolled individuals who are **currently or formerly served by Child Welfare**:
- Children and youth **currently in foster care** (any placement type)
- **Former foster care youth** up to age 26 (aging-out youth)
- **Young adults** up to age 26 who were in foster care
- **Minor children of eligible youth** (children of former foster youth under 26)

**Auto-enrollment**: ~32,000 eligible individuals were automatically enrolled upon December 1, 2025 launch.

## What CFSP Covers

CFSP provides the most comprehensive benefit package in NC Medicaid:

### Physical Health (same as Standard Plan)
All Standard Plan physical health services — preventive care, primary care, specialist referrals, hospital, prescriptions.

### Behavioral Health (enhanced over Standard Plan — includes most Tailored Plan BH services)
- Outpatient therapy (individual, group, family)
- Inpatient psychiatric
- Crisis services (mobile crisis, crisis stabilization)
- Therapeutic residential options (children)
- Enhanced behavioral health and residential services
- 1915(i) Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS)

### Health-Related Social Needs (HRSN)
CFSP is also responsible for addressing **unmet health-related resource needs**, including:
- Housing
- Food
- Transportation
- Interpersonal violence

This is especially important for foster youth transitioning to independence who face housing instability and food insecurity.

## Key Features

### Statewide Continuity of Care
A critical problem for foster children historically: when placements change counties, providers often change too. CFSP solves this by:
- Being a **statewide plan** — not region-specific like LME-MCO Tailored Plans
- Allowing beneficiaries to continue with the same providers even if they move to a different county within NC
- Coordinating across child welfare placements

### Integrated Care Management
CFSP integrates:
- Physical health care management
- Behavioral health care coordination
- Child welfare system coordination (DSS/county agencies)
- Trauma-informed care

### 1915(i) HCBS Services
Unlike Standard Plans (which cover 1915(c) waivers only through LME-MCOs), CFSP covers **1915(i) state plan HCBS** — providing more flexible home and community-based services without waiver slot limitations.

## CFSP vs. Other NC Medicaid Plans

| Feature | Standard Plan | BH I/DD Tailored Plan | CFSP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Target pop | General Medicaid | SMI/SUD/I/DD/TBI | Foster care / child welfare |
| BH services | Mild-moderate | Full spectrum | Full spectrum (most Tailored) |
| HRSN | Via HOP (suspended) | Some | Yes — core responsibility |
| 1915(c) Innovations | No | Yes | Via CFSP |
| 1915(i) HCBS | No | No | Yes |
| Statewide | Yes | No (by region) | Yes |
| Managed by | Commercial PHPs | LME-MCOs | Blue Cross NC |

## Policy Context

The CFSP launch addresses longstanding critiques that foster children on Medicaid received fragmented, inconsistent care because they were assigned to region-based Standard Plans or LME-MCOs that changed when placements moved. NC Health News (December 2025) reported that the new plan "aims to untangle care for NC foster children."

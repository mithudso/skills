# NC Medicaid Dental and Vision Coverage

## Overview

North Carolina Medicaid has long covered dental and vision services for **children** (as required by federal law under EPSDT). With the **Medicaid Expansion effective December 1, 2023**, approximately 600,000 newly eligible adults gained access to dental, vision, and hearing benefits as part of the NC Medicaid comprehensive benefit package.

## Adult Dental Benefits

### What's Covered

NC Medicaid covers these dental services for adult beneficiaries:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Diagnostic** | Exams, X-rays (periapical, bitewing, panoramic) |
| **Preventive** | Cleanings (prophylaxis), fluoride treatments, sealants |
| **Restorative** | Fillings (amalgam and composite), crowns (limited) |
| **Endodontics** | Root canals (anterior, bicuspid) |
| **Periodontics** | Scaling and root planing (gum disease treatment) |
| **Prosthodontics** | Dentures (complete and partial), denture repairs |
| **Oral Surgery** | Extractions, biopsy, complex surgical extractions |

### Expansion Impact on Dental (Dec 2023–2025)
- **628,681+** adults enrolled by early 2025
- **$28.6 million+** in dental claims in the first 7 months after expansion
- Newly eligible adults: ages 19–64, ≤138% FPL

## Access Challenges: The Dentist Participation Gap

Despite coverage, significant access barriers persist:

- **~60% of NC dentists** do not accept Medicaid patients
- Many who do accept Medicaid are not taking new patients
- **Reimbursement rate**: 34 cents on the dollar — same as 2008; NC Medicaid dental reimbursement has not been updated in 15+ years
- NC legislators proposed higher Medicaid reimbursement rates for dentists in 2025 (Feb 2025 legislation)

### Geographic Disparities
- Rural counties: fewer participating dentists per capita
- FQHCs fill some gap (340B drug pricing supports dental at FQHCs)
- Mobile dental programs serve some rural/school populations

## Vision Benefits

NC Medicaid covers vision services for adults:
- Routine eye exams
- Eyeglasses (frames and lenses) — one pair per year (or medically necessary)
- Contact lenses (when medically necessary)
- Low vision aids

For children: covered under **EPSDT** (Early and Periodic Screening, Diagnostic and Treatment), which includes more comprehensive vision services.

## Hearing Benefits

NC Medicaid covers:
- Audiological evaluations
- Hearing aids (for children under EPSDT; adults: more limited)
- Cochlear implant evaluation and fitting

## Delivery Through Managed Care

Dental benefits are typically carved out from Standard Plan and Tailored Plan benefits and delivered through a **dental benefits manager (DBM)**. NC Medicaid Direct (FFS) also has dental coverage. Members access dental through:
- Standard Plan-contracted dental providers
- FQHCs (integrated dental at many sites)
- Safety-net dental clinics
- Dental schools (UNC School of Dentistry, ECU Dental School, etc.)

## Children's Dental: EPSDT

Children under 21 receive dental services under **EPSDT (Early and Periodic Screening, Diagnostic and Treatment)**:
- More comprehensive than adult benefits
- Required by federal law
- Includes comprehensive oral exams, orthodontics (when medically necessary), fluoride varnish at medical visits
- School-based dental sealant programs in many NC counties

# Medicare Advantage (Part C) Enrollment

Medicare Advantage (Part C) is an alternative to Original Medicare delivered by private insurers approved by CMS; it bundles Part A + B (and usually Part D) into one plan with added benefits.

## Prerequisite

Must be enrolled in both Medicare Part A and Part B and live in the plan's service area. Original Medicare enrollment comes first — you cannot join a Medicare Advantage plan without it.

## Enrollment Periods

| Period | Dates | What You Can Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Initial Enrollment Period (IEP)** | 7-month window around 65th birthday | Enroll in Medicare Advantage for the first time |
| **Annual Enrollment Period (AEP)** | Oct 15 – Dec 7 each year | Join, switch, or drop a Medicare Advantage plan; coverage effective Jan 1 |
| **Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (MA OEP)** | Jan 1 – Mar 31 each year | Switch Medicare Advantage plans or return to Original Medicare; one change only |
| **Special Enrollment Period (SEP)** | Triggered by qualifying event | Moving out of service area, losing employer coverage, gaining/losing dual eligibility, plan contract ends |
| **5-Star SEP** | Dec 8 – Nov 30 | Enroll in a 5-star Medicare Advantage plan once per calendar year anytime |

## How to Enroll

1. Go to **medicare.gov/plan-compare** or call 1-800-MEDICARE to compare plans by ZIP code.
2. Confirm your Medicare card shows Part A + Part B effective dates.
3. Enroll online, by phone, or by mailing the plan's enrollment form during a valid enrollment period.
4. Provide your Medicare number and Part A/B coverage start dates.
5. Coverage for AEP selections begins January 1 of the following year.

## Plan Selection Criteria

- **Network**: HMO (must use network, needs referrals) vs. PPO (flexibility, higher cost out-of-network) vs. PFFS, SNP
- **Drug formulary**: Check if current medications are covered at what tier
- **Star ratings**: CMS rates plans 1–5 stars annually; 4+ = high quality
- **Extra benefits**: Dental, vision, hearing, fitness (99% of 2026 plans include vision; 97% include dental/hearing)
- **Out-of-pocket maximum**: Plans must cap annual out-of-pocket costs; compare caps
- **Premium**: Many plans have $0 premium but review cost-sharing structure

## Switching Between Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare

- During AEP (Oct 15–Dec 7): Drop Medicare Advantage and return to Original Medicare with a Part D plan
- During MA OEP (Jan 1–Mar 31): Can switch back to Original Medicare once
- Returning to Original Medicare does not guarantee Medigap enrollment without underwriting outside the 6-month Medigap OEP

## Special Needs Plans (SNPs)

- **D-SNPs**: For dual eligibles (Medicare + Medicaid)
- **C-SNPs**: For people with specific chronic conditions (ESRD, diabetes, heart disease, etc.)
- **I-SNPs**: For institutional residents (nursing facilities)
- Must meet the SNP's specific eligibility criteria to join

## 2026 Plan Changes

CMS requires plans to submit bids annually; benefits can change year to year. Review your Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) each September — sent by current plan — to compare coverage changes before AEP.

## Common Mistakes

- Forgetting to check if preferred doctors/specialists are in-network
- Not comparing drug formularies before enrolling
- Missing the MA OEP window (Jan–Mar) to make a mid-year correction
- Assuming Medicare Advantage disenrollment automatically restores Medigap coverage (it does not guarantee it)

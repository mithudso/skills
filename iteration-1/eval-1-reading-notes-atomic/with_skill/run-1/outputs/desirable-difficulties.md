# Desirable difficulties

**Desirable difficulties** (Bjork): harder encoding produces stronger retention. Difficulty introduced during learning — the kind that feels counterproductive in the moment — can strengthen later recall.

Source: Bjork (via Make It Stick, Brown et al.).

# Retrieval practice beats re-reading

Testing yourself beats re-reading. The act of recalling — **retrieval practice** — strengthens memory more than re-exposure to the material does.

Evidence: Karpicke & Blunt (2011, *Science*) found retrieval practice beat elaborative studying (concept mapping) for learning science texts — even though students predicted the opposite.

Sources: Make It Stick (Brown et al.); Karpicke & Blunt 2011 (*Science*).

# NC Health Insurance Premium Payment (HIPP) Program

## Overview

The **NC Health Insurance Premium Payment (HIPP) Program** is a NC Medicaid program that pays the cost of **employer-sponsored or COBRA health insurance premiums** for Medicaid members when doing so is cost-effective for the Medicaid program.

**Website**: [www.MyNCHIPP.com](https://www.mynchipp.com/)

**Administered by**: NC Medicaid / Division of Health Benefits (DHB), NCDHHS

## How HIPP Works

1. A Medicaid member has access to employer-sponsored health insurance (through their job or a family member's job, or COBRA)
2. HIPP evaluates whether Medicaid paying the insurance premium would **cost less than** what Medicaid would spend directly on the member's healthcare
3. If cost-effective: HIPP **pays the insurance premiums** (employee share and sometimes the employer share)
4. The member **keeps Medicaid** coverage AND uses the employer plan as their **primary insurance**
5. Medicaid pays remaining cost-sharing as secondary payer

**Key feature**: The member gets dual coverage — employer plan (primary) + Medicaid (secondary) — at no cost to the member.

## Eligibility

### Who qualifies:
- Active NC Medicaid members (any coverage group)
- Must have access to employer-sponsored group health insurance or COBRA
- Insurance must meet the cost-effectiveness test (HIPP saves Medicaid money overall)

### Priority populations (high-cost conditions that make HIPP more cost-effective):
- Asthma
- Diabetes
- Epilepsy / seizure disorders
- Cardiac disease
- Renal failure / kidney disease
- Cancer
- HIV/AIDS
- Other high-cost chronic conditions

The program has **broadened eligibility** over time to include more Medicaid recipients with these high-cost conditions.

## Cost-Effectiveness Test

HIPP performs a calculation:
- **Cost to pay premiums** for the member + family (if applicable)
- vs. **Cost of covering all Medicaid-covered services** directly

If premium cost < projected Medicaid service cost → HIPP is cost-effective → premiums paid.

This is not a burden for the member to calculate — HIPP staff conduct the analysis.

## What HIPP Covers

- Employee-share premium payments
- In some cases, employer-share premiums
- COBRA premium payments (for qualifying members who have COBRA rights)

HIPP does **not** pay for:
- Individual market (ACA marketplace) plans
- Plans not offered through an employer/COBRA

## Application Process

1. Apply through [MyNCHIPP.com](https://www.mynchipp.com/) (electronic or printable application)
2. Provide employer insurance information and cost details
3. HIPP staff conduct the cost-effectiveness analysis
4. If approved: HIPP pays premiums directly to the insurance carrier
5. Member uses employer plan as primary insurance; Medicaid as secondary

## Coordination with Managed Care

For Medicaid members enrolled in a **Standard Plan** or **Tailored Plan**:
- HIPP coordination with managed care plan is facilitated by NCTRACKS (NC Medicaid's claims system)
- Employer plan processes claims first; Medicaid handles remaining cost-sharing
- Members should notify their Medicaid managed care plan of HIPP enrollment

## Why HIPP Matters for High-Cost Members

For members with serious chronic conditions, employer insurance may cover services that Medicaid would otherwise pay for at higher cost. HIPP can:
- Give members access to a broader provider network (employer plans often have broader networks than Medicaid managed care)
- Reduce Medicaid expenditures
- Ensure continuity of care with established providers

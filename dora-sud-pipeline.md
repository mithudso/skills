# Dual Orexin Receptor Antagonist (DORA) Pipeline in Substance Use Disorders

## Core Concepts

- Orexin (hypocretin) neurons in the lateral hypothalamus project to the VTA and nucleus accumbens, driving reward-seeking, stress reactivity, and arousal; overactivation underpins drug craving and withdrawal hyperarousal
- DORAs block both OX1R (stress/cue-reactivity loop) and OX2R (general arousal/sleep-wake); the dual block confers advantage over OX1R-selective compounds when withdrawal insomnia co-occurs
- FDA-approved DORAs (suvorexant, lemborexant, daridorexant) offer an immediate off-label clinical bridge while SUD-specific trials mature
- The SUD rationale is strongest at two phenotypic clusters: (1) AUD + comorbid insomnia in early withdrawal, and (2) stimulant/opioid use disorder with hyperarousal-driven relapse
- Schedule IV DEA classification limits prescribing flexibility but is compatible with addiction-medicine settings

## Mechanisms / Evidence

- **Suvorexant in AUD (NCT03897062):** Australian RCT terminated due to COVID-related recruitment failure and Merck ceasing trial-supply; one documented participant showed complete abstinence over 13-week blinded treatment and self-reported 93% craving reduction (2024 case report, Campbell et al., *Clinical Case Reports*)
- **Suvorexant in MUD (case series, Dec 2024 PubMed):** Preliminary data in methamphetamine use disorder patients; preclinical basis is suvorexant reducing MA self-administration and MA-induced reinstatement in rat models
- **Stimulant-use DORA RCT (JMIR Protocols 2026):** Open-label proof-of-concept trial began enrollment July 2024; 8 participants enrolled by September 2025, target N=40, expected completion January 2027; outcome: sleep, stress, and craving during early stimulant withdrawal
- **Lemborexant in OUD (NCT04818086):** Phase 1b/2a DDI study examining lemborexant as adjunctive treatment with buprenorphine/naloxone; safety focus on sedation overlap and CYP3A4 interactions
- **SB-649868 (GSK, discontinued):** Preclinical DORA with dual OX1R/OX2R potency; entered clinical insomnia evaluation ~2005 but halted; no SUD-specific human data; mechanistic benchmark only
- **OX1R-selective compounds (SB-334867, ACT-335827):** Reduce cue-induced reinstatement selectively but lack OX2R-mediated sleep improvement, making them less suitable when insomnia co-drives relapse risk

## Clinical Application

- Best-fit patient: AUD or stimulant/opioid SUD + documented insomnia (ISI ≥ 10) + hyperarousal-pattern withdrawal; suvorexant 10–20 mg at bedtime is the most evidence-adjacent off-label choice
- Avoid or titrate cautiously when patient is on buprenorphine or methadone (CYP3A4 substrate overlap; lemborexant DDI trial addresses this gap); contraindicated with narcolepsy
- Monitor for next-day sedation (Epworth ≥ 10 is a stopping signal), complex sleep behaviors, and worsening depression; these concerns are lower than with benzodiazepine alternatives used in withdrawal
- Regulatory status: no FDA-approved SUD indication for any DORA as of 2026; off-label use is supported by case-level evidence and preclinical data but not Phase 2 RCT results yet

## Key Facts

- NCT03897062 terminated; COVID + Merck supply withdrawal; yielded only 1 documented responder
- Stimulant-use DORA proof-of-concept trial enrolling 2024–2027, N=40 target (JMIR Protocols, 2026)
- Suvorexant reduces MA self-administration and reinstatement in rodent models (preclinical basis for MUD use)
- Lemborexant DDI study with buprenorphine (NCT04818086) specifically addresses OUD adjunct safety

# PACE: Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly

## What Is PACE?

PACE (Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly) is a managed care program providing comprehensive medical and social services to frail elderly individuals who qualify for nursing home-level care but prefer to live in the community. PACE is both a Medicare Advantage Special Needs Plan and a Medicaid managed care program.

**Key concept:** PACE allows elderly individuals who qualify for nursing home care to remain in their homes and communities with comprehensive, interdisciplinary support.

**Legal basis:** Balanced Budget Act 1997 (BBA); 42 CFR Part 460

## Eligibility Requirements

To enroll in PACE, an individual must meet ALL of:

1. **Age:** 55 or older (most PACE enrollees are in their 70s–80s)
2. **Care level:** Certified as needing nursing-facility-level care by the state (requires functional assessment — typically 2–3 ADL limitations or equivalent cognitive/behavioral needs)
3. **Community-dwelling:** Live in the PACE service area and be able to safely live in the community with PACE support (not currently requiring 24/7 institutional care)
4. **Geography:** Live within a PACE organization's service area

**Important note on disability/age:** PACE requires age 55+, but does not require Medicare eligibility. An individual 55–64 who is not yet Medicare-eligible may enroll if they qualify for Medicaid.

## Financing and Enrollment

**Dual eligible (Medicare + Medicaid):** Most PACE enrollees are dual-eligible.
- Medicare capitation payment covers Medicare services
- Medicaid capitation payment covers Medicaid services
- PACE receives combined per-person monthly capitation from both programs
- Enrollees pay no premiums or copays for covered services

**Medicaid-only (under 65 or not Medicare-eligible):** Pay monthly Medicaid capitation only; no premium if income below Medicaid limit.

**Private pay:** Individuals above Medicaid income may pay privately (monthly fee for full PACE benefit package). Expensive but provides comprehensive care.

## PACE Benefits Package

PACE provides ALL Medicare and Medicaid covered services, plus additional services, all through one program:

**Medical care:**
- Primary care (PACE physician is primary care provider)
- Specialist consultations
- Hospital care (inpatient)
- Emergency care
- Pharmacy / prescription drugs
- Lab, X-ray, diagnostics

**Long-term services and supports (LTSS):**
- Adult day health center (typically 3–5 days/week) — hub of PACE model
- Personal care (help with bathing, dressing, grooming at home)
- Home health care
- Nursing care
- Occupational, physical, speech therapy

**Social and supportive services:**
- Meals (at PACE center and sometimes home delivery)
- Social work
- Transportation to and from PACE center and medical appointments
- Recreation / activities

**No separate premiums or copays** for dual-eligible enrollees.

## The PACE Day Center Model

The adult day health center is central to PACE:
- Enrollees attend multiple days per week
- Receive medical care, therapies, meals, social activities, and personal care at the center
- Interdisciplinary team (physician, nurse, PT/OT, social worker, nutritionist, activity therapist) meets weekly to coordinate each enrollee's care
- Transportation provided to/from center

## Disenrollment

PACE enrollment is voluntary. Enrollees can disenroll at any time:
- Upon disenrollment: must return to Original Medicare + Medicaid or choose other managed care
- PACE disenrollment may trigger need to choose new Medicare Advantage or Part D plan

Reasons for involuntary disenrollment (rare):
- Requires 24/7 nursing home care PACE cannot safely provide
- Geographic move out of service area
- Non-payment (private-pay enrollees)

## PACE vs Other Dual-Eligible Programs

| Feature | PACE | D-SNP | FIDE-SNP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age | 55+ | Any Medicare-eligible | Any Medicare-eligible |
| Care level | Nursing-home eligible required | No requirement | No requirement |
| Day center | Yes (cornerstone) | No | No |
| Benefit integration | Full (all in one org) | Partial-full | Full |
| Geography | PACE service area | Nationwide (varies) | Varies |
| Capitation | Both Medicare + Medicaid | Medicare capitation | Both (where fully integrated) |

## PACE Availability in NC

PACE is not available everywhere — it requires a PACE organization to operate in your area.

**NC PACE organizations (as of 2025):**
- **Pace Advantage** (Charlotte area): Mecklenburg County
- **ElderCare PACE** (Research Triangle): Wake, Durham, Johnston counties
- **Mountain PACE** (Western NC): Buncombe, Henderson counties

**Limited availability:** Large portions of NC, particularly rural areas, do not have PACE organizations. This limits access for many who would qualify.

**NC Medicaid:** NC covers PACE as a Medicaid-covered managed care option; PACE organizations in NC have agreements with NC DHHS Division of Health Benefits.

## Referral and Enrollment Process

1. Referral from physician, hospital social worker, or self-referral to local PACE organization
2. PACE intake assessment — home visit + functional evaluation
3. State Medicaid nursing-home-level-of-care determination
4. Enrollment agreement signed
5. Coverage begins next month (or sooner in some organizations)

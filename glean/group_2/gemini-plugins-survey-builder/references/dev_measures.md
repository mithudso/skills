# Developer Experience & Productivity Survey Frameworks

This document outlines the most robust and widely adopted frameworks for measuring developer experience (DevEx) and productivity. Each section includes example survey items (Likert scale or open-ended) that can be used in internal developer sentiment surveys.

---

## 1. The SPACE Framework
The SPACE framework provides a multidimensional view of productivity, ensuring that speed is not optimized at the expense of wellbeing or quality.

### Example Survey Items:
* **Satisfaction & Wellbeing:** "I feel burned out from my work." (Reverse scored)
* **Performance:** "I am satisfied with the quality of the code my team produces."
* **Activity:** "On a typical day, how many hours do you spend in meetings versus coding?"
* **Communication & Collaboration:** "It is easy to find the right person to answer my technical questions."
* **Efficiency & Flow:** "I am able to complete my work with minimal interruptions."

---

## 2. The DevEx Framework (Three Pillars)
Focuses on the "developer journey" and the friction encountered in the daily workflow. This is currently the gold standard for identifying internal bottlenecks.

### Example Survey Items:
* **Feedback Loops:** "How satisfied are you with the time it takes to get a code review from a peer?"
* **Cognitive Load:** "How easy is it to find the information needed to complete a task in our documentation?"
* **Flow State:** "How often are you able to reach a state of 'flow' (deep concentration) during the workday?"

---

## 3. DORA (Software Delivery Performance)
While usually pulled from system data (Git, Jira), perception surveys help validate if the system metrics match the developer's reality.

### Example Survey Items:
* **Deployment Frequency:** "On average, how frequently does your team deploy code to production?"
* **Change Failure Rate:** "In the last quarter, what percentage of your team's deployments resulted in a service impairment?"
* **Lead Time:** "How much time does it usually take for code to go from 'merged' to 'live in production'?"

---

## 4. AI-Augmented Developer Experience (New for 2025/26)
As AI tools become standard, measuring their impact on productivity and satisfaction is critical.

### Example Survey Items:
* **AI Utility:** "To what extent do AI coding assistants (e.g., Copilot) reduce the time you spend on boilerplate tasks?"
* **Skill Growth:** "The AI tools provided by the company are helping me learn new patterns and languages faster."
* **Mental Effort:** "Using AI tools has reduced the mental effort required to debug complex issues."

---

## 5. Developer Thriving & Vitality
A modern construct focusing on long-term career health and psychological safety.

### Example Survey Items:
* **Psychological Safety:** "If I make a mistake on this team, it is not held against me."
* **Growth:** "I feel I am gaining new skills that are relevant to my career goals."
* **Tooling Satisfaction:** "The tools I have at my disposal allow me to do my best work."

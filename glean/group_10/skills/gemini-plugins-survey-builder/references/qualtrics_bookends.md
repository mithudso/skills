# Qualtrics Survey Bookends: Guidelines & Templates

This document provides standardized templates and guidelines for the introductory and concluding sections of MongoDB research surveys.

---

## 1. Introduction Instructions
The introduction sets expectations for the participant. It should clearly state the purpose, estimated time, and any compensation or raffle incentives.

### Guidelines
* **Time Estimate:** Always provide an honest estimate of the time required.
* **Value Proposition:** Briefly explain how their feedback impacts the product.
* **Placeholders:** Ensure compensation details are updated per project requirements.

### Template Text
> Thank you for taking the time to participate in our survey 😃
>
> This survey should take approximately **[Time, e.g., 15 minutes]** to complete and will ask about your preferences and needs regarding **[Subject, e.g., MongoDB and AI]**. Your feedback is very valuable to our product and research teams. The insights you share will help us better understand our users and find ways to improve our products and services.
>
> **[Compensation/Raffle Placeholder Section]:**
> *At the end, you’ll have the opportunity to enter a raffle to win [Incentive Details, e.g., one of 35 digital rewards ($250, $100, or $50)]. Entry is optional — full details and terms provided at the end of the survey.*
>
> We appreciate your contribution.

---

## 2. End of Survey: Research Panel Invitation
Before the final "Thank You" screen, include a call-to-action (CTA) to join the MongoDB research pool. This helps build a long-term panel of engaged users.

### Template Text
**Question Text:**
Want to help shape the future of MongoDB? Join our research panel [https://www.mongodb.com/user-research](https://www.mongodb.com/user-research) and get occasional invites to give feedback on new features, designs, and experiences to improve MongoDB for the entire developer community.

Opportunities might include:
* Short surveys
* Early feature previews
* 1:1 chats with our product and design teams

Participation is always optional and often offers a thank-you gift.

**Response Options (Single Select):**
* [ ] Yes, I would like to learn more about future research and feedback opportunities.
* [ ] No, not at this time.

## 3. Implementation Checklist
- [ ] Update the **Time Estimate** in the intro.
- [ ] Define the **Subject Matter** in the intro.
- [ ] Customize the **Compensation** placeholder or remove it if no incentive is offered.
- [ ] Ensure the **Research Panel link** is clickable in the Qualtrics editor.
- [ ] Set up **Display Logic** if you only want to show the research panel invite to specific segments.

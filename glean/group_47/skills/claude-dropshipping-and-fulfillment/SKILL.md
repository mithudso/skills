# Dropshipping and Fulfillment

Sibling of `ecommerce-fundamentals` (general fulfillment models), `subscription-commerce` (billing/churn), and `ecommerce-automation-and-tools` (order-routing/inventory-sync tooling).

## Quick reference — supplier/tooling decision

| Need | Tool/Supplier | Note |
|---|---|---|
| General product dropshipping from China | CJ Dropshipping, AliExpress (via DSers) | Longer shipping (1-4 weeks typical unless using a China-based fulfillment-partner warehouse) |
| US/EU faster-shipping suppliers | Spocket, AutoDS | Higher per-unit cost, shorter transit time |
| Print-on-demand (custom-print apparel/goods) | Printful, Printify | No inventory risk at all; unit cost is highest of the three |
| Order routing/automation | DSers, AutoDS, Printful/Printify native Shopify apps | Automates supplier order placement on each sale |

## Quick reference — margin structure

| Line item | Dropship | Print-on-demand |
|---|---|---|
| Typical gross margin | 15-30% | 20-40% |
| Main margin risk | Price competition (same supplier, many sellers) | Base-product cost inflation |
| Shipping-time risk | High (long transit hurts reviews/refunds) | Moderate (POD prints-to-order, adds days) |

# Sources & Further Reading

Full bibliography for the `effective-altruism-and-philanthropic-decision` skill.

## Foundational texts

1. **Singer, P.** (1972). "Famine, Affluence, and Morality." *Philosophy and Public Affairs*, 1(3), 229–243. The drowning-child argument establishing the moral duty to give.

2. **MacAskill, W.** (2015). *Doing Good Better: Effective Altruism and a Radical New Way to Make a Difference*. Guardian Faber. Primary EA introductory text; introduces SNT framework.

3. **MacAskill, W.** (2022). *What We Owe the Future*. Basic Books. The longtermism case; argues future persons' welfare deserves equal moral weight.

4. **Ord, T.** (2020). *The Precipice: Existential Risk and the Future of Humanity*. Hachette. Existential-risk prioritization and longtermist philanthropy.

## Cost-effectiveness methodology

5. **GiveWell.** (2025). "Cost-Effectiveness Analyses — November 2025 version." givewell.org/how-we-work/our-criteria/cost-effectiveness/cost-effectiveness-models/november-2025-version. Primary methodology documentation.

6. **GiveWell.** (n.d.). "Cost-Effectiveness." givewell.org/how-we-work/our-criteria/cost-effectiveness. Overview including moral weights explanation and funding bar.

## Head-vs-heart tension

7. **Small, D.A., Loewenstein, G., & Slovic, P.** (2007). "Sympathy and callousness: The impact of deliberative thought on donations to identifiable and statistical victims." *Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes*, 102(2), 143–153. Key finding: analytic priming reduces emotional giving without raising statistical-victim giving.

## Cause prioritization & career guidance

8. **80,000 Hours.** (2016/2026). "A framework for comparing global problems in terms of expected impact" and "Why and how to earn to give." 80000hours.org. SNT framework operationalization and earning-to-give guidance.

9. **Wiblin, R.** (2016). "A framework for comparing global problems." 80,000 Hours. Detailed SNT application with personal-fit dimension.

## Longtermism critiques

10. **Schwitzgebel, E.** (2024, June). "The Meta-Epistemic Objection to Longtermism." Substack. Presented at Oxford Global Priorities Institute. Argues billion-year EV calculations are epistemically unjustifiable.

11. **Mounk, Y.** (2024, September). "The Problem with Effective Altruism." Substack. Critique of EA's shift from near-term to longtermist priorities.

12. **Schliesser, E.** (2024, April). "On Shallow Ponds and Effective Altruism." Crooked Timber. Structural justice critique; EA brackets historical injustice and reparations.

## Post-FTX & EA movement critiques

13. **Matthews, D.** (2022, December). "How effective altruism let Sam Bankman-Fried and the FTX collapse happen." *Vox: Future Perfect.* Structural analysis of EA culture failures.

14. **EA Forum.** (2024, December). "Effective Altruism, Neglectedness and Public Choice Theory." forum.effectivealtruism.org. SNT institutional critique; discusses lock-in and cause neutrality.

15. **Singer, P.** (2024, July). Peter Singer AMA. EA Forum. Singer's current views on longtermism emphasis and movement priorities.

## Definitional references

16. **MacAskill, W.** (2015/2025 revised). "The Definition of Effective Altruism." Centre for Effective Altruism. Canonical two-part definition.

17. **Christian Science Monitor.** (2022, December). "Wider fallout from a crypto collapse: How should charity really be done?" csmonitor.com. Balanced post-FTX coverage including GiveWell moral-weights discussion.

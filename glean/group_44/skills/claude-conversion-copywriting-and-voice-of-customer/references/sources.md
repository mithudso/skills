# Sources and References

Full annotated citation list for the conversion-copywriting-and-voice-of-customer skill.

1. **Wiebe, Joanna.** "Amazon Review Mining for Copywriting." Copyhackers, Oct 2014. https://copyhackers.com/2014/10/amazon-review-mining/ — foundational review mining methodology; identifying the right corpus; messaging hierarchy outputs.

2. **Noel, Jessica.** "7 Copywriting Research Techniques Conversion Copywriters Love." Copyhackers, Jun 2022. https://copyhackers.com/2022/06/copywriting-research/ — overview of VOC research methods; client interviews as tier-1 source; review mining without interviewer effect.

3. **Havice, Jennifer.** "How to Use Voice of Customer Research to Boost Conversions." CXL, Aug 2015. https://cxl.com/blog/voice-of-customer/ — survey-based VOC; the Wiebe rehab-center headline case study (400%+ CTA lift); message hierarchy construction from survey data.

4. **Wiebe, Joanna (for CXL).** "How to Write Copy That Sells Like a Mofo." CXL, Jan 2016. https://cxl.com/conversion-rate-optimization/how-to-write-copy-that-sells-like-a-mofo-by-joanna-wiebe/ — five-step messaging hierarchy process; synthesis over summary; split-testing as the final arbiter.

5. **Laja, Peep.** "How to Come Up with More Winning Tests Using Data [ResearchXL Model]." CXL, Apr 2015. https://cxl.com/blog/how-to-come-up-with-more-winning-tests-using-data/ — seven-step research model with copy testing as Step 7; ICE prioritization; hypothesis format (Craig Sullivan).

6. **Green, David.** "A/B Split Testing: How to Use the Right Test Progression." MarketingExperiments, Aug 2015. https://marketingexperiments.com/value-proposition/ab-split-testing-how-to-use-the-right-test-progression-to-get-customer-discoveries-and-results — radical redesign → variable cluster → single-factor test sequence.

7. **MarketingExperiments.** "Headlines on Deadlines: How to Consistently Write Effective Headlines." MECLABS, Dec 2011. https://marketingexperiments.com/value-proposition/methodology-to-effective-headlines — headline evaluation framework (appeal, credibility, exclusivity, clarity).

8. **MarketingExperiments.** "Value Proposition: How Headlines Helped Lead to a Nearly 29% Conversion Boost." MECLABS, Aug 2011. https://marketingexperiments.com/value-proposition/value-proposition-headline — 10-headline test; all value-statement headlines outperformed no-headline control.

10. **Palmer, Rob.** "Breakthrough Advertising: Schwartz for Modern Funnels." RobPalmer.com, Feb 2026. https://robpalmer.com/blog/eugene-schwartz-breakthrough-advertising-lessons — applied Schwartz for modern funnel and email contexts; awareness vs. sophistication distinction.

11. **Bolton, Anna.** "Voice-of-Customer Copywriting Research: An Overview." ConversionCopyCo, Sep 2020. https://conversioncopyco.com/voice-of-customer-copywriting-research-overview/ — gold-standard three-source method (surveys + interviews + comment mining); before/during/after story framework.

15. **CXL Institute.** "The Data-Driven Conversion Optimization Guide." CXL / Texonica, 2022. https://texonica.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/The-Conversion-Optimization-Guide-CXL.pdf — hypothesis format (Craig Sullivan); copy testing methodology; ICE scoring; test progression.

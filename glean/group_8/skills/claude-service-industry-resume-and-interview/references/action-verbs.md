# Action Verbs and Vocabulary — Service Industry Resumes

## Weak vs. Strong Comparison

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Responsible for bar" | "Managed 15-seat bar generating $12k/night revenue" |
| "Served customers" | "Served 200+ patrons nightly using Toast POS, maintaining 99.8% customer satisfaction" |
| "Made drinks" | "Mixed 180–220 drinks per shift across 14-seat bar top and 3 service wells" |
| "Helped with inventory" | "Cut inventory shrinkage 14% through weekly BinWise audits" |
| "Good at upselling" | "Boosted average order value $17/ticket through suggestive-selling scripts for seasonal cocktails" |

## Verb Categories

**Service/volume**: Served, Mixed, Prepared, Poured, Plated
**Revenue/sales**: Upsold, Boosted, Generated, Increased, Drove
**Operations**: Managed, Maintained, Restocked, Audited, Reduced
**Leadership**: Trained, Led, Coordinated, Supervised
**Compliance/safety**: Verified, Enforced, Refused, Ensured
**Guest experience**: Resolved, Greeted, Cultivated, Personalized

## Hospitality-Specific Language That Signals Competence

Use industry vocabulary that shows insider knowledge:

- "high-volume" (signals pace tolerance)
- "covers" (industry term for guest counts — use it; casual phrasing like "tables" reads as FOH-basic)
- "suggestive selling" or "upsell scripts" (shows technique, not just luck)
- Named POS systems: Toast, Aloha, Square, Micros, SpotOn
- Named reservation platforms: OpenTable, Resy, SevenRooms
- Named inventory tools: BinWise, BlueCart
- Specific spirits/wine vocabulary where applicable: "amaro selection," "Japanese whisky program," "natural wine list"

Avoid corporate euphemisms ("synergized guest experience") — they read as AI-generated filler in this context.

## Tailoring Vocabulary by Venue Type

A craft cocktail bar and a chain sports bar require different keyword stacks:

| Venue | Vocabulary to Lead With |
|-------|------------------------|
| Craft cocktail | house-made tinctures, amaro flights, fat-washing, clarification, original recipes, technique depth |
| Chain restaurant bar | Aloha POS, upsell compliance, menu consistency, ticket time, modifiers |
| Hotel bar | brand-standard compliance, formal service standards, wine program, TIPS certified |
| High-volume nightclub | throughput, covers per bartender per shift, composure under pressure, accuracy |
| Dive / neighborhood pub | regulars, relationship-building, community knowledge, warmth |

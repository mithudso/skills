# Medicare Savings Programs (MSPs)

> **Educational only, NOT legal or financial advice.** Income limits change
> annually and vary by state. Apply through your **state Medicaid office**
> or call **1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227)**.
> **verified-as-of: 2026-06-22**

Medicare Savings Programs are **state-administered, federally funded programs**
that help low-income Medicare beneficiaries pay Medicare premiums, deductibles,
and cost-sharing. They are administered through Medicaid agencies but serve
Medicare beneficiaries who may not otherwise qualify for full Medicaid. Enrollment
in certain MSPs **automatically qualifies you for Extra Help** (the Part D
Low Income Subsidy) without a separate application.

---

## Part 1 — The four MSPs at a glance

| Program | What Medicaid pays | 2026 approx. income limit (individual) | 2026 approx. income limit (couple) |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **QMB** — Qualified Medicare Beneficiary | Part A + Part B premiums + deductibles + coinsurance | ~$1,350/month | ~$1,824/month |
| **SLMB** — Specified Low-Income Medicare Beneficiary | Part B premium only | ~$1,616/month | ~$2,184/month |
| **QI** — Qualifying Individual | Part B premium (partial or full) | ~$1,816/month | ~$2,455/month |
| **QDWI** — Qualified Disabled and Working Individuals | Part A premium only | ~$4,945/month | ~$6,658/month |

> Limits are approximations based on 2026 Federal Poverty Level (FPL) guidelines;
> exact amounts vary by state and are updated annually. Some states have more
> generous limits. Verify with your state Medicaid office.

---

## Part 2 — QMB (Qualified Medicare Beneficiary)

QMB is the most comprehensive MSP. If enrolled in QMB, Medicaid pays:
- Your **Part A premium** (if you would otherwise have to pay one)
- Your **Part B premium** ($202.90/month in 2026)
- Part A and Part B **deductibles** ($1,736 Part A per benefit period; $283 Part B annual)
- Medicare **coinsurance and copays**

### The QMB billing protection

**Critically important:** Medicare providers (doctors, hospitals) are **prohibited
by law** from billing QMB enrollees for Medicare cost-sharing (deductibles, copays,
coinsurance). This applies even to providers that do not accept Medicaid. If a
provider bills you for Medicare cost-sharing and you are a QMB beneficiary, you
do not owe it — report the billing to 1-800-MEDICARE.

This billing protection is frequently violated and many QMB beneficiaries are
billed incorrectly. You have the right to dispute and refuse payment for Medicare
cost-sharing charges.

### Auto Extra Help

QMB enrollment **automatically qualifies you for Extra Help** (the Part D Low
Income Subsidy), reducing drug copays to small amounts ($1.55–$4.65 range
per prescription in 2026 at full LIS). No separate Extra Help application needed.

---

## Part 3 — SLMB (Specified Low-Income Medicare Beneficiary)

SLMB pays the **Part B premium only** ($202.90/month in 2026). It does not cover
deductibles or coinsurance. Income limits are slightly above QMB.

Like QMB, SLMB enrollment **automatically qualifies you for Extra Help** for
Part D drug coverage.

---

## Part 4 — QI (Qualifying Individual)

QI pays the Part B premium (all or a portion). Income limits are slightly above
SLMB. Key differences from QMB/SLMB:
- **Must reapply each year** — QI is not automatically renewed
- Applications are approved on a **first-come, first-served basis** until
  annual funding runs out; apply early in the year
- QI also confers **automatic Extra Help** for Part D

---

## Part 5 — QDWI (Qualified Disabled and Working Individuals)

QDWI is for people under 65 who:
- Have a disability
- Are working
- Lost premium-free Part A when they returned to work
- Have income up to approximately 200% FPL

QDWI pays the **Part A premium** (up to $565/month in 2026) so working people
with disabilities can maintain Medicare coverage. Does not cover Part B costs
or confer automatic Extra Help.

---

## Part 6 — Asset limits

Most MSPs have asset tests (excluding primary home, one car, personal belongings,
and burial funds). **2026 approximate asset limits:**

| Program | Individual | Couple |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| QMB | ~$9,660 | ~$14,470 |
| SLMB | ~$9,660 | ~$14,470 |
| QI | ~$9,950 | ~$14,910 |

> **IRA change for Extra Help (not MSPs):** The Inflation Reduction Act (2022)
> eliminated the asset test for the Part D **Extra Help** program starting 2024
> — but asset limits remain for the MSPs themselves. Check if your state has
> adopted more generous asset limits.

---

## Part 7 — How to apply

1. **Contact your state Medicaid office** — MSPs are administered by states;
   find your state's office at medicaid.gov
2. **Apply online, by phone, or in person** — applications accepted at Social
   Security Administration offices for QI; state Medicaid office for QMB/SLMB
3. **Documents needed:** proof of Medicare enrollment, income documentation
   (SS award letter, pay stubs, benefit statements), asset documentation,
   proof of residency
4. **Enrollment is year-round** (unlike Medicare Advantage enrollment windows)
5. **If denied:** request a hearing; Medicaid fair hearing rights apply

---

## Part 8 — How MSPs interact with dual eligibility

Being in a Medicare Savings Program does not, by itself, make you "dually
eligible" in the full sense. True dual eligibility (full Medicaid + Medicare)
provides far more benefits, including coverage for long-term services and supports.

MSPs are specifically for Medicare beneficiaries who need help with Medicare
cost-sharing but who may not qualify for full Medicaid. The QMB/SLMB/QI
programs represent a lower Medicaid-funded benefit specifically tied to
reducing Medicare's cost-sharing burden.

See `medicare-medicaid-dual-eligibility` for the full dual-eligibility framework.

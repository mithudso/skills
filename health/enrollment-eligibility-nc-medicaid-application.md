# NC Medicaid Application Process

## NC Medicaid Expansion (October 2023)

North Carolina became one of the last states to expand Medicaid, with expansion taking effect **October 1, 2023** under the Medicaid expansion provision of the Affordable Care Act (ACA §1396a).

**New eligibility under NC expansion:**
- Adults aged 19–64 with household income up to 138% FPL (~$20,783/year individual; ~$35,632/year family of 3 in 2025)
- Estimated 600,000+ newly eligible North Carolinians
- Covers adults who previously fell in the "coverage gap" (income too high for traditional Medicaid, too low for ACA subsidies)

## NC Medicaid Eligibility Groups

| Group | Income Limit | Asset Test |
|---|---|---|
| Adults 19–64 (expansion) | 138% FPL | None |
| Children 0–18 | 216% FPL (NC CHIP) | None |
| Pregnant women | 196% FPL | None |
| Families/parents | Varies by household | None (MAGI) |
| Elderly/disabled (non-MAGI) | SSI-related standard | Yes (~$2,000) |
| Family Planning Only | 195% FPL | None |

## How to Apply

### Option 1: NC FAST Online Portal (Preferred)
- URL: epass.nc.gov (NC FAST — Families Accessing Services through Technology)
- Available 24/7; can save and return to application
- Can apply for Medicaid, SNAP, TANF, and other benefits simultaneously
- Account creation required; identity verification during process
- Application confirmation number provided on submission

### Option 2: Local DSS Office (In-Person)
- Apply at county Department of Social Services
- 100 county DSS offices statewide
- Documents collected and scanned on-site
- Helpful for complex cases, non-MAGI groups, long-term care

### Option 3: Phone
- NC Medicaid call center: 1-888-245-0179
- Telephone Signature available for some applicants
- Limited capacity; wait times can be significant

### Option 4: Mail/Fax
- Paper application available from NCDHHS or county DSS
- Mail to local DSS or NC Medicaid Processing Center

### Option 5: Authorized Representative / Helper
- Hospital financial counselors ("presumptive eligibility" workers)
- Medicaid assisters and Navigators (especially for expansion group)
- Federal Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs)
- Legal aid attorneys

## Required Documentation

**Identity:**
- Driver's license, state ID, passport, birth certificate, or other photo ID

**Citizenship/Immigration:**
- US birth certificate, US passport, Certificate of Naturalization
- For immigrants: Green Card, visa documentation

**Residency:**
- NC address (utility bill, lease, or attestation)
- No minimum residency duration

**Income:**
- Pay stubs (last 30 days), W-2s, tax returns
- SSA award letter (for Social Security/SSDI/SSI)
- Self-employment: profit/loss statement, business records
- Self-attestation may be accepted initially; verified electronically

**For non-MAGI (elderly/disabled):**
- Bank statements (last 3 months)
- Investment account statements
- Life insurance policies (for cash value assessment)
- Property documentation

## Processing Timelines

| Group | Federal Standard |
|---|---|
| Most MAGI applicants | 45 days |
| Disability-based (non-MAGI) | 90 days |
| Pregnant women (emergency) | As fast as possible |
| Presumptive Eligibility | Same day |

## NC Medicaid Managed Care Structure

As of July 1, 2021, most NC Medicaid enrollees are in **NC Medicaid Managed Care** (not fee-for-service):

**Standard Plans (mainstream population):**
- AmeriHealth Caritas NC
- Healthy Blue (BCBS NC)
- Molina Healthcare of NC
- United Healthcare Community Plan
- WellCare of NC

**Tailored Plans (for individuals with complex behavioral health needs):**
- Serve members with serious mental illness, substance use disorder, intellectual/developmental disabilities, traumatic brain injury
- Operated by Local Management Entities / Managed Care Organizations (LME-MCOs)
- Members auto-assigned or self-select
- Behavioral health services more integrated

**Enrollment choice:** After approval, members have 30 days to select a Standard or Tailored Plan. Default assignment occurs if no choice made.

## Presumptive Eligibility (PE)

Hospitals, FQHCs, and certain agencies can determine "presumptive eligibility" for Medicaid — a provisional determination that provides immediate temporary coverage while the full application is processed.

- For pregnant women, children, former foster youth
- Expansion adults: presumptive eligibility also available
- PE worker makes determination on-site; coverage begins immediately
- Full application must be submitted within 10 days for coverage to continue

## NC Medicaid and SUD Treatment

Key for NC drug rehabilitation context:
- Medicaid covers MAT (buprenorphine, methadone through OTPs, XR-naltrexone)
- NC Medicaid covers OTP (methadone clinic) services
- Tailored Plans handle BH integration for SUD + mental health
- NC Medicaid covers SBIRT (Screening, Brief Intervention, Referral to Treatment)
- Peer support services covered under NC Medicaid

## Fair Hearing / Appeals

- Written denial/termination notice required with reason and appeal rights
- Fair hearing request: within 90 days of notice
- Aid Continuing: existing benefits continue during appeal if timely request
- State Office of Administrative Hearings handles Medicaid appeals in NC
- Legal Aid of North Carolina provides free representation for low-income applicants

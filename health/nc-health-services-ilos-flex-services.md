# NC Medicaid In-Lieu-of-Service (ILOS) and Value-Added Services

## Overview

Within NC Medicaid's managed care framework, **Standard Plans and BH I/DD Tailored Plans** have flexibility to offer two categories of services beyond the standard Medicaid benefit package:

1. **In-Lieu-of-Services (ILOS)** — substitutes for state plan services that are more cost-effective or clinically appropriate
2. **Value-Added Services (VAS)** — extra benefits offered by plans that go beyond what the state plan covers

These tools allow NC Medicaid managed care organizations to tailor benefits to member needs, particularly for addressing **health-related social needs (HRSN)** — the social determinants (food, housing, transportation) that drive health outcomes.

## In-Lieu-of-Services (ILOS)

### Definition

ILOS are **medically appropriate and cost-effective substitutes** for covered Medicaid state plan services. A plan may offer an ILOS instead of the state plan service when:
- The ILOS is medically appropriate for the member
- The ILOS is cost-effective compared to the state plan service
- The member agrees to receive the ILOS

**Classic example**: A community-based depression screening in lieu of an office visit depression screening. The result is clinically equivalent but delivered in a more accessible, lower-cost setting.

### ILOS for Health-Related Social Needs

NC, like a growing number of states, has used ILOS authority to cover **non-medical services** that address HRSN under CMS waiver authority. This enables NC Standard Plans to include in their capitation:
- Food security interventions (medically tailored meals, produce prescriptions)
- Transitional housing supports
- Transportation to medical appointments
- Safety-related interventions

NC is identified among states with 1115 demonstrations that address health-related social needs and use ILOS as part of their overall HRSN strategy.

### Federal Regulatory Framework (2024)

**CMS May 2024 final rule** significantly strengthened ILOS:
- Officially went into effect **July 9, 2024**
- Codified the 5% cap: **ILOS costs cannot exceed 5% of total capitation payments**
- States with ILOS costs under 1.5% face less reporting/evaluation burden
- ILOS for HRSN must be approved by CMS
- Members accessing ILOS have the **same appeals and grievance rights** as for standard Medicaid services

### 2025 Federal Policy Retreat

In March 2025, CMS **rescinded two Informational Bulletins** that had laid out options for covering HRSN through ILOS. This created uncertainty about the permissibility and scope of HRSN-focused ILOS going forward. NC Medicaid's existing ILOS arrangements were grandfathered, but expansion plans were affected.

## Value-Added Services (VAS)

Value-Added Services are **extra benefits plans offer voluntarily** beyond what the state plan requires and beyond ILOS. Unlike ILOS, VAS are typically **not included in capitation rates** — plans fund them from administrative savings or as a competitive differentiator.

Examples in NC Standard Plans may include:
- Enhanced dental or vision benefits
- Fitness/wellness programs
- Nurse hotlines with expanded hours
- Care management services beyond state requirements
- Transportation coordination beyond covered medical trips

## ILOS Under NC Medicaid Special Populations

NC explicitly includes ILOS in capitation rates **for members with:**
- Serious Mental Illness (SMI)
- Severe Substance Use Disorders (SUD)
- Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities (I/DD)
- Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)

This reflects recognition that these high-need populations have elevated HRSN and benefit from non-medical service supports included in managed care arrangements.

## Relationship to NC's HRSN Strategy

NC's overall HRSN approach operates at multiple levels:

| Program/Tool | Mechanism | Who Administers |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy Opportunities Pilots (HOP) | 1115 waiver; Medicaid pays for SDOH services | NCDHHS + CBOs (suspended July 2025) |
| ILOS | MCO flexibility; substitutes for state plan services | Standard Plans / Tailored Plans |
| VAS | Plan-offered extras; not capitation-funded | Standard Plans |
| NCCARE360 | Referral infrastructure; tracks social needs | NCDHHS / FHLI |
| NC Medicaid SDOH screening | Mandatory screening under managed care contracts | Plans (all enrolled members) |

## Practical Implications for Beneficiaries

NC Medicaid beneficiaries enrolled in a Standard Plan may be eligible for ILOS or VAS benefits **that vary by plan**. Plans are required to communicate their ILOS and VAS offerings to members. Beneficiaries should ask their plan directly about:
- What food security services are available
- Whether transportation to non-medical appointments is covered
- What mental health support services are offered beyond therapy visits
- Whether housing support services are available

# MSLT Protocol Limitations in Substance Use Populations

## Core Concepts

- AASM standard: 2-week washout for stimulants, stimulant-type drugs, and REM-sleep-suppressant medications before MSLT
- REM rebound after abrupt cessation of REM-suppressants produces false-positive SOREMPs that mimic narcolepsy Type 1/2 signature (≥2 SOREMPs)
- Heavy/chronic methamphetamine use causes persistent dopamine transporter downregulation extending well beyond 2 weeks, making standard washout inadequate
- Adherence to washout is poor: only ~6% of patients on REM-suppressant agents actually discontinue before testing
- ICSD-3 exclusion criterion: symptoms "not better explained by medication/substance use" — substance confounders must be ruled out before a diagnosis stands

## Mechanisms / Evidence

- REM rebound mechanism: chronic stimulant/antidepressant use suppresses REM pressure; abrupt withdrawal triggers compensatory REM rebound detectable for weeks post-cessation
- Meth-specific: dopaminergic neurotoxicity and DAT downregulation recover over 12–24 months (neuroimaging data); sleep architecture disruption — including orexin-circuit dysregulation — persists well past 2 weeks in heavy users
- False-positive SOREMP rates in stimulant/antidepressant withdrawal contexts are clinically significant; short mean sleep latency (<8 min) plus ≥2 SOREMPs can be produced entirely by withdrawal-state REM rebound, not narcolepsy pathology
- AASM 2021 protocol guidance acknowledges long-half-life agents (e.g., fluoxetine, armodafinil) may require washout up to 6 weeks — the 2-week standard is a floor, not a ceiling
- Actigraphy + sleep diary for ≥7 days prior to MSLT is required to confirm stable sleep-wake schedule; in active or recent substance users this also captures sleep fragmentation that independently shortens sleep latency
- CSF orexin-A (hypocretin-1) ≤110 pg/mL is pathognomonic for NT1 and is substance-independent — the only biomarker not confounded by drug state

## Clinical Application

- **Decision algorithm for recent stimulant use:** If washout < 4–6 weeks (heavy/chronic users) or < 2 weeks (light/intermittent), defer MSLT; obtain CSF orexin-A if cataplexy is present or clinical suspicion is high
- **Extended washout threshold:** For patients with ≥6 months heavy stimulant use (especially methamphetamine), consider minimum 4-week washout with clinical judgment for longer; document rationale
- **Actigraphy requirement:** Confirm ≥7 nights of adequate total sleep time (>6 hrs/night) before MSLT; persistent sleep fragmentation from withdrawal confounds mean sleep latency independently of SOREMPs
- **Urine drug screen on MSLT day:** Mandatory; positive screen invalidates results for any stimulant, cannabis (THC suppresses REM), or benzodiazepine/hypnotic
- **When MSLT cannot be deferred:** Document substance history fully; interpret SOREMPs with caution; CSF orexin-A provides diagnostic clarity if result is ≤110 pg/mL

## Key Facts

- AASM standard washout: **2 weeks** for stimulants; up to **6 weeks** for long-half-life REM suppressants (fluoxetine)
- Adherence rate to pre-MSLT washout: **~6%** of patients on relevant agents in practice
- CSF orexin-A cutoff: **≤110 pg/mL** (or <1/3 of mean normal) = NT1; not affected by drug state
- Meth DAT recovery timeline: structural/functional recovery requires **12–24 months** after cessation in heavy users — far exceeding any MSLT washout guideline

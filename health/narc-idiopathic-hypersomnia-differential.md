# Idiopathic Hypersomnia Differential

## Core Concepts
- **IH definition**: Central hypersomnia with mean MSLT sleep latency ≤8 min, <2 SOREMPs, and no secondary cause; characterized by unrefreshing naps and severe sleep inertia ("sleep drunkenness")
- **IH vs NT2**: Sole MSLT distinction is SOREMP count — NT2 requires ≥2 SOREMPs; IH requires 0–1; mean sleep latency threshold (≤8 min) is identical for both
- **MSLT-negative IH**: Subset with subjective EDS, prolonged nighttime sleep (≥11 hr/24 hr), severe sleep inertia but normal MSLT (>8 min); ICSD-3-TR recognizes this via 24-hour sleep monitoring criterion
- **ADHD-EDS overlap**: Inattention and daytime drowsiness shared across IH, NT1/NT2, and ADHD; MSLT cluster analysis (Frontiers Psychiatry 2024) shows distinct temporal MSLT patterns separating ADHD from central hypersomnia
- **Prevalence**: IH estimated 0.01–0.02% population; diagnosis-to-treatment lag often 10+ years; NT2 shares similar diagnostic delay trajectory

## Mechanisms / Biology
- **CSF somnogen hypothesis**: A small bioactive peptide identified in CSF of IH patients allosterically potentiates GABA-A receptor function, increasing sensitivity to endogenous GABA — the "somnogen" produces hypersomnia by tonically enhancing inhibitory tone in arousal circuits
- **GABA-A sensitization**: Unlike benzodiazepines (orthosteric), the IH somnogen acts at a distinct modulatory site; effect is reversible by flumazenil (GABA-A antagonist) and normalized by clarithromycin (negative allosteric modulator via GABA-A)
- **Norepinephrine & histamine deficits**: IH associated with decreased CSF histamine and norepinephrine dysregulation — distinct from NT1's orexin/hypocretin deficiency (CSF orexin normal in IH)
- **Arousal circuit dysfunction**: IH reflects impaired wake-promoting drive (locus coeruleus, tuberomammillary nucleus, basal forebrain) rather than REM-system instability seen in narcolepsy
- **ADHD neurobiological overlap**: Both IH and ADHD involve prefrontal hypodopaminergia; stimulants treat EDS in both, complicating differential — ADHD medications may mask undiagnosed IH

## Clinical Picture
- **Hallmark triad**: Excessive daytime sleepiness (despite ≥7 hr nocturnal sleep), unrefreshing prolonged naps, and sleep inertia lasting 30–60+ minutes post-awakening
- **IH vs NT2 clinically**: NT2 patients more likely to have refreshing naps, hypnagogic hallucinations, sleep paralysis; IH patients have longer, unrefreshing naps and more severe morning sleep inertia
- **ADHD mimicry**: EDS-driven inattention meets DSM-5 ADHD criteria in many IH/NT patients; hyperactivity exploratory study (PMC 2023) found ADHD-like symptoms in ~30–40% of narcolepsy and IH patients
- **Diagnostic delay burden**: Systematic review (Sleep Advances 2024) documents median 8–10 year delay from symptom onset to IH diagnosis; frequent prior misdiagnosis as depression, ADHD, or chronic fatigue
- **Onset**: Typically adolescence to early adulthood; no cataplexy, HLA-DQB1*06:02 association weak (unlike NT1)

## Evidence & Treatment
- **Low-sodium oxybate (Lumryz/Xywav)**: FDA-approved 2021 specifically for IH — phase 3 REST-IH trial: significant reduction in Epworth Sleepiness Scale vs placebo (withdrawal design, p<0.0001); only IH-specific approval
- **Clarithromycin pharmacoprobe**: 500 mg BID; 2012 pilot and 2013 retrospective review showed ≥60–70% of GABA-related hypersomnia patients had subjective improvement; mechanism — negative allosteric modulation of GABA-A receptor normalizes somnogen-enhanced inhibition
- **Flumazenil pharmacoprobe**: IV/sublingual flumazenil improved psychomotor vigilance and alertness in 7 hypersomnolent patients across IH, NT2, and long-sleeper phenotypes (Rye et al.); bioavailability limits clinical use; compounded sublingual used off-label
- **Modafinil**: Only agent with strong guideline recommendation (AASM); typical doses 200–400 mg/day; treats EDS but does not address sleep inertia
- **Pitolisant, solriamfetol**: Off-label for IH; pitolisant (H3 antagonist, wake-promoting) used when stimulants inadequate; traditional stimulants (methylphenidate, amphetamines) used off-label with guideline acknowledgment

## Key Facts
- IH MSLT criterion: mean sleep latency ≤8 min with 0–1 SOREMPs (vs NT2's ≥2 SOREMPs at identical ≤8 min threshold)
- CSF orexin is **normal** in IH (>110 pg/mL); low CSF orexin (<110 pg/mL) definitively diagnoses NT1
- REST-IH trial: low-sodium oxybate reduced ESS score by ~4.7 points vs placebo withdrawal; first FDA-approved IH treatment (2021)
- Clarithromycin 500 mg BID response rate ~60–70% in CSF somnogen-positive patients; antibiotic effect is pharmacological, not antimicrobial
- Sleep inertia duration >30 min distinguishes IH from most other EDS disorders and is a key ICSD-3-TR criterion

# NC LME-MCOs: The Four Regional Behavioral Health Managers

## What is an LME-MCO?

**Local Management Entities / Managed Care Organizations (LME-MCOs)** are state-contracted regional organizations that manage mental health, substance use disorders, and intellectual/developmental disability (MH/SUD/I/DD) services for NC Medicaid beneficiaries and uninsured/underinsured individuals.

LME-MCOs:
- Authorize and pay for BH/SUD/I/DD services within their region
- Operate 24/7 crisis lines and crisis services
- Manage provider networks
- Operate the NC Tailored Plans (for members with serious needs)
- Provide state-funded services for those who don't qualify for Medicaid

## 2023–2024 Consolidation

NC DHHS ordered major consolidation, reducing LME-MCOs from **six to four**:

| Former entities | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Sandhills Center | Dissolved Nov 2023; counties redistributed to Alliance, Partners, and Trillium |
| Eastpointe | Merged into Trillium Health Resources (2024) |
| Cardinal Innovations | Merged into Alliance Health prior to 2023 consolidation |
| Partners Health Management, Trillium, Alliance Health, Vaya Health | Four remaining LME-MCOs (absorbed new counties) |

By 2024, the four operating LME-MCOs are:

### 1. Alliance Health
- **Counties**: Wake, Durham, Johnston, Orange, Chatham, Lee, Cumberland (and others from Sandhills realignment)
- **Service population**: ~1.5 million
- **Tailored Plan**: Yes — Alliance Health Tailored Plan
- **Notable**: Largest single LME-MCO; manages Tailored Plans for Triangle/central NC

### 2. Partners Health Management
- **Counties**: Forsyth, Guilford, Davidson (from Sandhills), Rockingham, Harnett, and others
- **Service population**: Piedmont Triad region
- **Tailored Plan**: Yes — Partners Health Management Tailored Plan
- **Notable**: Absorbed Davidson County from Sandhills 2023

### 3. Trillium Health Resources
- **Counties**: Eastern NC (absorbed Eastpointe's counties + majority of Sandhills)
- **Service population**: ~60+ counties in eastern/southeastern NC
- **Tailored Plan**: Yes — Trillium Health Resources Tailored Plan
- **Notable**: Largest geographic footprint after Eastpointe merger

### 4. Vaya Health
- **Counties**: Western NC (mountain and foothills region, including Rockingham from Sandhills)
- **Service population**: Western NC including Hurricane Helene–affected areas
- **Tailored Plan**: Yes — Vaya Health Tailored Plan
- **Notable**: Manages Healthy Opportunities Pilot services in western NC region

## Functions of Each LME-MCO

1. **Medicaid Tailored Plan**: Integrated physical health + BH/SUD/I/DD for qualifying members
2. **State-funded services**: For uninsured/underinsured with serious MH/SUD/I/DD needs
3. **Crisis services**: 24/7 crisis lines, mobile crisis teams, crisis receiving facilities
4. **Access and authorization**: Prior authorization for behavioral health services
5. **Provider network management**: Contract with community providers, hospitals, residential facilities
6. **Innovations Waiver management**: Coordinate I/DD waiver services

## Finding Your LME-MCO

Members are assigned to an LME-MCO based on their county of residence. The [NC DHHS LME-MCO Directory](https://www.ncdhhs.gov/providers/lme-mco-directory) lists each county's assigned entity.

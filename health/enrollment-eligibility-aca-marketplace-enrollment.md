# ACA Marketplace Enrollment

## What Is the Marketplace?

The ACA established health insurance marketplaces (exchanges) where individuals and families can compare and purchase private health insurance plans. Two types:
- **Federally Facilitated Marketplace (FFM):** HealthCare.gov — used by most states (including NC)
- **State-Based Marketplaces (SBM):** 18 states + DC run their own (e.g., Covered California, NY State of Health, Connect for Health Colorado)

## Who Can Enroll

**Eligible:**
- US citizens and nationals
- Lawfully present immigrants (regardless of 5-year bar that applies to Medicaid)
- Not incarcerated
- Not eligible for Medicaid or CHIP (those found eligible are diverted to those programs)
- Not offered affordable employer coverage (for APTC purposes — specific affordability threshold)

**Not eligible for APTCs (but can purchase):**
- Those with access to affordable employer coverage
- Incomes > 400% FPL (eligible to purchase but historically received no subsidy — IRA changed this, see below)

## Enrollment Periods

### Open Enrollment Period (OEP)
- **Dates:** November 1 – January 15 each year (federal deadline; some SBMs extend)
- Coverage start:
  - Enroll by December 15 → coverage January 1
  - Enroll December 16 – January 15 → coverage February 1

### Special Enrollment Periods (SEPs)

Qualifying life events trigger 60-day SEP:

| Qualifying Event | SEP Window |
|---|---|
| Lost job-based or other qualifying coverage | 60 days after loss |
| Married | 60 days after event |
| Had a baby, adopted, placed foster child | 60 days after event |
| Moved to new coverage area (with prior coverage) | 60 days after move |
| Gained citizenship / immigration status | 60 days after gaining status |
| Released from incarceration | 60 days after release |
| Income change making newly eligible | 60 days |
| AmeriCorps, Peace Corps start/end | 60 days |
| Low income (< 150% FPL) | Monthly SEP (year-round, post-ARP) |

**SEP verification:** CMS requires documentation for most SEPs (proof of prior coverage loss, marriage certificate, etc.). Inconsistent verification was a fraud concern; verification strengthened 2016+ for most SEPs.

## Subsidies: Advanced Premium Tax Credits (APTCs)

**Who qualifies:** Household income 100%–400% FPL (expanded by IRA 2022 — see below).

**How it works:**
- Federal tax credit reduces monthly premium
- Can take in advance (Advanced PTC) to reduce monthly payments, or claim at tax time
- Credit reconciled at tax filing; if income higher than estimated → repay excess; lower → receive additional credit
- Must enroll in marketplace plan and file taxes

**APTC cliff (pre-IRA):** Income at 401% FPL → zero subsidy (the "subsidy cliff"). ARP 2021 temporarily eliminated; IRA 2022 extended through 2025 (pending further extension).

**Benchmark plan:** APTC = cost of Silver benchmark plan (second-lowest Silver) minus maximum contribution percentage.

**Maximum contribution percentages (2025, post-IRA rules):**
- 100–150% FPL: $0 (premium-free Silver benchmark)
- 150–200% FPL: 0–2%
- 200–250% FPL: 2–4%
- 250–300% FPL: 4–6%
- 300–400% FPL: 6–8.5%
- > 400% FPL: 8.5% cap (IRA change — no cliff)

## Cost-Sharing Reductions (CSRs)

**Who qualifies:** Income 100–250% FPL AND enrolled in a Silver plan.

**What CSRs provide:** Reduced deductibles, copays, and out-of-pocket maximums within Silver plans.

**Current status:** Federal CSR payments to insurers were terminated by executive order in 2017. Insurers still required to provide CSRs to eligible enrollees. Insurers now "Silver load" — increase Silver premiums to account for cost; this increases APTC amounts (since APTCs are tied to Silver benchmark). Net effect: some lower-income enrollees get better effective deals on Gold or Bronze plans.

## Income Verification

**How income is verified:**
1. Applicant self-attests projected annual household income
2. Federal Data Hub queries IRS (prior year tax data) and SSA
3. If inconsistency: applicant receives "data matching issue" (DMI) — must resolve within 90 days with documentation (pay stubs, employer letter, self-employment records)
4. Failure to resolve → loss of APTC

**Income to report:** MAGI — same definition as Medicaid MAGI (wages, net self-employment, Social Security, etc.)

**Household size:** IRS tax household rules (same as Medicaid MAGI)

## Citizenship and Immigration Verification

**US citizens:** Verify via SSA database
**Immigrants:** Verify via DHS SAVE system
**Data matching issues:** Applicant receives 90-day window to provide citizenship/immigration documentation

**Undocumented immigrants:** Not eligible for marketplace plans or APTCs. Emergency Medicaid only.

**DACA recipients (Dreamers):** As of 2024 rule, DACA recipients are now eligible for marketplace enrollment and APTCs (prior policy excluded them; Biden administration rule finalized May 2024 extended marketplace and APTC access to DACA). Subject to ongoing legal challenges.

## Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) 2022 Subsidy Extensions

**What IRA 2022 did:**
- Extended American Rescue Plan (ARP) 2021 temporary subsidy enhancements through 2025
- Eliminated 400% FPL cliff — APTCs now available at all income levels above 400% FPL (capped at 8.5% of income)
- Made Silver plans at 0% premium for 100–150% FPL (premium-free access)
- Net effect: largest marketplace enrollment since ACA passage; record 21+ million enrolled for 2024

**Post-2025 cliff risk:** Unless Congress acts again, IRA enhancements expire December 31, 2025. This would reinstate the 400% FPL cliff and reduce subsidies for millions.

## Plan Metal Tiers

- **Bronze:** ~60% actuarial value; lowest premium, highest cost-sharing
- **Silver:** ~70% AV; mid-range; required for CSR eligibility
- **Gold:** ~80% AV; higher premium, lower cost-sharing
- **Platinum:** ~90% AV; highest premium, lowest cost-sharing
- **Catastrophic:** <30 AV; for those under 30 or hardship exemption; no APTC allowed

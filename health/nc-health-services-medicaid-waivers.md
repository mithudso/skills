# NC Medicaid HCBS Waivers: CAP-DA, CAP-C, and Innovations

## Overview

North Carolina operates three major **1915(c) Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) waivers** that allow Medicaid to fund community-based care instead of institutional placement (nursing home, ICF/I/DD, etc.):

1. **CAP/DA** — Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults
2. **CAP/C** — Community Alternatives Program for Children
3. **NC Innovations Waiver** — for people with Intellectual/Developmental Disabilities

## 1. CAP/DA — Community Alternatives Program for Disabled Adults

**Target population**: Medically fragile or complex disabled adults age 18+, at risk of nursing facility placement.

**Key services**:
- Personal care services (ADL/IADL assistance)
- In-home aide services
- Respite care
- Adult day health services
- Home modifications (ramps, grab bars)
- Medical equipment and supplies
- Home-delivered meals
- Caregiver training

**Cost-effectiveness test**: CAP/DA services must cost less than what Medicaid would spend on nursing facility care.

**Status**: CAP/DA §1915(c) waiver authority expires periodically; as of 2024, renewal was in process (expiration Oct 31, 2024 per CMS records — NC DHHS typically secures renewal continuity).

## 2. CAP/C — Community Alternatives Program for Children

**Target population**: Children birth through age 20 who are medically fragile/complex, requiring an ICF or hospital level of care.

**Key services** (similar structure to CAP/DA but pediatric):
- Private duty nursing
- Respite care (in-home and facility-based)
- Home modifications
- Medical equipment
- Caregiver training
- Personal care services

**Distinguishing from Innovations Waiver**: CAP/C is for *medically fragile* children (complex physical conditions). Innovations is for children (and adults) with *intellectual/developmental disabilities*. A child can potentially qualify for both if they have both I/DD and medical fragility.

## 3. NC Innovations Waiver

**Target population**: North Carolinians of all ages with an **intellectual or developmental disability (I/DD)** or related condition who require an ICF/I/DD level of care.

**Renewal**: Renewed July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2029 (5-year renewal).

**Key services**:
- Community Living Supports (with new remote support options added 2024)
- Supported Living
- Supported Employment (revised 2024: Pre-employment, Employment Stabilization, Long-term Employment phases)
- Day Supports
- Residential Supports (group home, host home)
- Respite care
- Crisis services
- Assistive technology
- Home modifications

**Waitlist**: NC Innovations Waiver has a significant waitlist — thousands of individuals with I/DD are on the list. This is a major access barrier; the waiver is not an entitlement.

**Hurricane Helene flexibilities**: CMS approved temporary flexibilities for Innovations waiver members in western NC following Hurricane Helene, effective September 26, 2024 through June 30, 2025.

**Administration**: Managed by LME-MCOs on behalf of NC Medicaid. Tailored Plan operators (Alliance, Partners, Trillium, Vaya) integrate Innovations services into Tailored Plan benefits.

## 4. TBI Waiver

NC also operates a **Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) 1915(c) waiver** for adults with TBI requiring institutional-level care. Services include supported living, community integration, day programs. Integrated into BH I/DD Tailored Plans alongside Innovations.

## CAP/DA vs. CAP/C vs. Innovations: Key Differences

| Feature | CAP/DA | CAP/C | Innovations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Age | Adults 18+ | Birth–20 | All ages |
| Primary condition | Physical disability/medical | Medical fragility | I/DD |
| Level of care standard | Nursing facility | ICF or hospital | ICF/I/DD |
| Waitlist | Limited | Limited | Significant waitlist |

# Competency questions — visual-design-critique family

Family seeded by concept-family-explorer on 2026-06-16. Subject: visual design
(scoped to graphic/brand design + UI/UX product-screen critique). End goal: a
multi-stage design/image critique skill modeled on document-critique and the
*-deep-optimizer family.

Each question is something a person working this family should be able to ask
and reach a skill. Saturation (Step 8 coverage condition) requires every CQ to
resolve to a HAVE or researched node.

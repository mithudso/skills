# Competency questions — FSI / financial regulation / bank compliance / govcloud family

*Seeded 2026-06-11 by concept-family-explorer (subject: Goldman Sachs, FSI,
financial industry regulation, bank compliance, govcloud). Audience: a MongoDB
TAM / database-vendor field engineer serving a G-SIB. Each question should
resolve to an installed skill or researched concept-tree node.*

- Which US and UK/EU regulators examine a G-SIB like Goldman Sachs, and what do their examination regimes mean for cloud and database workloads?
- What does DORA require of a bank's ICT third-party providers, and how do its obligations (register of information, TLPT, incident reporting) land on a database vendor?
- What records-retention rules (SEC 17a-4 / FINRA WORM, MiFID II record-keeping) apply to data in a database, and what storage/architecture features satisfy them?
- What are FedRAMP authorization levels, AWS GovCloud constraints, DoD impact levels (IL4/IL5), and FIPS 140-3 validation — and when does a financial institution or its public-sector business need them?
- What evidence does a bank's third-party risk management (TPRM) team request from a SaaS/database vendor (SOC 2, ISO 27001, pen-test summaries, BCP/DR attestations, exit plans), and how should a TAM navigate that review?
- What do cloud-outsourcing rules (EBA outsourcing guidelines, PRA SS2/21, MAS outsourcing notices) require for material bank workloads — audit rights, exit strategy, concentration risk, resilience testing?
- What operational-resilience obligations (UK PS21/3 impact tolerances, DORA digital resilience testing, Fed/OCC/FDIC sound practices) drive a bank's failover and DR testing cadence, and how should a vendor support those tests?
- What encryption and key-management posture (BYOK/HYOK, customer-managed keys, HSM custody, rotation cadence) do bank compliance teams require for cloud data at rest and in transit?
- What data-residency and cross-border transfer constraints (GDPR/Schrems II, sovereign-cloud regimes, localization laws) govern where a bank's data and backups may live?
- How is AI/GenAI use governed inside a bank (SR 11-7 model risk, EU AI Act, NYDFS guidance) when vendor AI features — vector search, auto-embedding, LLM assistants — enter the architecture?

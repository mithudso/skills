# Study-technique utility rankings (Dunlosky 2013)

The Dunlosky et al. (2013) review rated common study techniques by utility:

- **High utility:** practice testing, distributed practice.
- **Low utility:** highlighting, re-reading.
- **Middling:** summarization.

Source: Dunlosky 2013 review.

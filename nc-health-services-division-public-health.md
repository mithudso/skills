# NC Division of Public Health (DPH)

## Overview

The **NC Division of Public Health (DPH)** is the state health agency within NCDHHS responsible for population-level disease prevention, health promotion, and health protection. It works through 85 **local health departments (LHDs)** and statewide partnerships to protect the health of all 10+ million North Carolinians.

**Mission**: Promote disease prevention, health services, and health promotion programs that protect communities from communicable diseases, epidemics, and contaminated food and water.

## Organizational Structure (Key Sections)

### Women, Infant, and Community Wellness Section (WICWS)
Reorganized 2024; three branches:
1. **Maternal Health Branch** — prenatal care, maternal mortality review, safe sleep, perinatal regionalization
2. **Reproductive Health Branch** — family planning, STI prevention/treatment, contraceptive access
3. **Infant and Community Health Branch** — infant mortality, newborn screening, early intervention linkage

### Chronic Disease and Injury Section (CDIS)
- Cardiovascular disease prevention
- Cancer prevention and early detection
- Tobacco prevention and cessation
- Diabetes prevention and management
- Injury prevention (falls, motor vehicle crashes, violence)
- Stroke and heart attack response systems

### Communicable Disease Branch / Epidemiology
- Disease surveillance and outbreak investigation
- Immunization program (VFC — Vaccines for Children)
- HIV/STI surveillance and partner services
- Tuberculosis control
- Vector-borne disease (ticks, mosquitoes)
- Healthcare-associated infections

### Environmental Health Section
- Food and lodging inspections
- On-site wastewater (septic systems)
- Water quality (private wells, recreational water)
- Lead and hazardous waste exposure

### Public Health Preparedness and Response (PHP&R)
- Emergency preparedness planning with hospitals and LHDs
- Medical countermeasure distribution (SNS)
- Public health emergency response (hurricanes, chemical releases, pandemics)
- Health Alert Network

## Title V Maternal and Child Health Program

NC's **Title V program** (federally funded, state administered) is housed within DPH's WICWS. It:
- Sets state priorities for maternal and child health outcomes
- Funds evidence-based programs through LHDs and CBOs
- Reports annually to HRSA on NC MCH performance measures
- Collaborates across NCDHHS divisions (Medicaid, CDSA, etc.)

Key 2024 priority areas: maternal mortality (especially Black maternal mortality), preterm birth, oral health in children, ACEs, adolescent health.

## WIC — Women, Infants, and Children

NC WIC provides nutritional support, breastfeeding support, and referrals to:
- Pregnant women, postpartum women, infants and children up to age 5
- ≤185% FPL
- Administered through DPH via local health departments
- ~250,000 NC participants monthly

## Local Health Departments (LHDs)

North Carolina has **85 local health departments** (covering all 100 counties — some are district health departments serving multiple counties). They:
- Provide clinical preventive services (immunizations, family planning, STI testing)
- Conduct food/environmental inspections
- Respond to disease outbreaks
- Implement state DPH programs locally

## NC Public Health Accreditation

Many NC LHDs pursue **Public Health Accreditation Board (PHAB)** accreditation. NC was one of the first states to pursue statewide accreditation.

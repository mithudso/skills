# GS — Business Structure & Engineering Org

Public-source. ✅ fact · 🔶 inference · ⚠️ verify first.

## Segments (post-2022 reorg) ✅

- **Global Banking & Markets (GBM)** — core institutional: trading, dealmaking, FICC/equities.
- **Asset & Wealth Management (AWM)** — includes former Marcus consumer deposits. **Highest-value MongoDB target.**
- **Platform Solutions** — being wound down.

🔶 Tech-buying is re-centering on **institutional (GBM)** and **AWM** platforms, not consumer fintech.

## Consumer-business exit (strategic shift) ✅

- **Apple Card → Chase**, announced **2026-01-07**. Solomon: this "substantially completes the narrowing of our focus in our consumer business."
  - Source: goldmansachs.com press release (2026-01-07); bankingdive.com.
- GM card → Barclays (2024-10); GreenSky sold (2023-10); Marcus Invest → Betterment.

## Engineering scale & culture ✅

- **~12,000 developers/engineers** — ~25% of a ~46,000 total workforce. Self-describes as "a technology company."
  - Source: cnbc.com (2025-07-11); aol.com (Argenti interview).
- **Core Engineering** = central platform group of **3,000+** — data centers, cloud, networking, storage, IAM, APIs, middleware.
  - Source: aol.com (Argenti interview).

⚠️ The 12,000 / 3,000+ / 46,000 figures recur across 2025–2026 sources but headcounts shift; the 3,000+ Core Engineering number rests on a single interview.

🔶 A large internal-platform org → GS often **builds vs buys** and standardizes through central platform teams.

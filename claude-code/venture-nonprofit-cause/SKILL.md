# NC Nonprofit & Cause Venture (hub)

The front door for a **North Carolina nonprofit / cause founder**, built around an organ-donation-awareness venture but general to any NC charitable cause. This hub routes to 6 reference spokes. Its **sibling hub, `venture-business`,** owns the for-profit entity, tax, payroll, real-estate, and marketing side.

**When activated:** match the question to a row, then **Read the listed `references/` file** before answering at depth (the table is only a router). Everything here is **educational information, not legal, tax, or financial advice**; rules and figures change and are date-stamped *as of 2026* — verify with the NC Secretary of State, NCDOR, IRS, HRSA/OPTN, or a licensed professional before acting.

## Cross-cutting notes

- **Sibling hub — `venture-business`.** The for-profit founder toolkit: NC entity formation/tax, payroll, entity lifecycle, business planning, investor pitch decks & financial models, real estate, and general/local-SEO marketing. The investor-deck spoke there also carries the **nonprofit grant-writing lifecycle** (LOI, proposal, logic model, grant budget). Start here for the charitable side.
- **Donor psychology is a neighbor, not here.** *Why* people give (warm glow, identifiable-victim, compassion fade), fundraising-practice psychology, and volunteer motivation live in `psychology-of-charitable-giving`, `fundraising-and-donor-psychology`, and `applied-psychology`. This hub owns the **operational** cause/nonprofit work.
- **Sensitive domain.** Organ-donation content is ethics-forward and educational only — not medical advice, and not policy advocacy beyond describing the landscape. Donor stories require informed, revocable consent (see `venture-donor-community-engagement`).
